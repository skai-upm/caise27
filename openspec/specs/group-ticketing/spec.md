# group-ticketing Specification

## Purpose

Lets groups buy tickets jointly: a group ticket is composed of one individual ticket per member, priced with a member-count discount, and locks the group while it is current or future.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Create group ticket
`GROUPTICKET CREATE;id;groupId;yyyy-MM-dd` SHALL create a group ticket for an existing group with at least one member, for a date not earlier than the system date, generating one individual ticket per member at the current individual price, and print `OK GROUP TICKET CREATED -> <total>`.

#### Scenario: Successful purchase
- **WHEN** group G01 has 3 members, the price is 40.00 and `GROUPTICKET CREATE;TG01;G01;2026-10-15` is processed
- **THEN** a group ticket with 3 individual tickets is created and `OK GROUP TICKET CREATED -> 108.00` is printed

#### Scenario: Empty or unknown group
- **WHEN** the group does not exist or has no members
- **THEN** an `ERROR` result is printed and nothing is created

### Requirement: Group composition at purchase time
The individual tickets of a group ticket SHALL correspond to the group's members at the moment of purchase; later membership changes SHALL NOT alter an existing group ticket. Generated individual tickets SHALL reference their users like ordinary individual tickets.

#### Scenario: Member removed after a past purchase
- **WHEN** a group ticket was bought for 3 members and, once it is past, one member is removed from the group
- **THEN** the group ticket still contains 3 individual tickets

### Requirement: Discount brackets
The total price SHALL be the sum of the individual prices minus a discount by member count: fewer than 3 → 0%; 3–5 → 10%; 6–10 → 20%; more than 10 → 50%.

#### Scenario: Bracket boundaries at 40.00 per ticket
- **WHEN** group tickets are bought for 2, 3, 5, 6, 10 and 11 members
- **THEN** the totals are 80.00, 108.00, 180.00, 192.00, 320.00 and 220.00

### Requirement: Locked group
While a group has any group ticket dated on or after the system date, members SHALL NOT be added or removed and the group SHALL NOT be deleted. When all its group tickets are past, it SHALL be modifiable again.

#### Scenario: Locked group rejects changes
- **WHEN** G01 has a group ticket dated 2026-10-15 and the system date is 2026-10-15
- **THEN** `GROUP ADD`, `GROUP REMOVE` and `GROUP DELETE` on G01 print an `ERROR` result and change nothing

#### Scenario: Lock released
- **WHEN** the system date advances to 2026-10-16
- **THEN** G01 can be modified and deleted again

### Requirement: Internal tickets block user deletion
Individual tickets generated by a group ticket SHALL block deletion of their users while dated on or after the system date, like ordinary individual tickets.

#### Scenario: Member with current group ticket
- **WHEN** Ana is a member of a group ticket dated 2026-10-15 and the system date is 2026-10-10
- **THEN** `USER DELETE` for Ana prints an `ERROR` result

### Requirement: Delete group ticket
`GROUPTICKET DELETE;id` SHALL delete a group ticket only when its date is later than the system date: first the passes of each of its individual tickets, then those individual tickets, and finally the group ticket, printing `OK GROUP TICKET DELETED`. A rejected deletion SHALL leave the group ticket, its individual tickets and their passes unchanged.

#### Scenario: Future group ticket
- **WHEN** TG01 is dated 2026-10-15 and the system date is 2026-10-14
- **THEN** `GROUPTICKET DELETE;TG01` removes TG01 and its individual tickets

#### Scenario: Current or past group ticket
- **WHEN** TG01 is dated 2026-10-15 and the system date is 2026-10-15
- **THEN** an `ERROR` result is printed and nothing is removed

#### Scenario: Passes of members deleted in cascade
- **WHEN** future TG01 is deleted while its individual tickets have SpeedPasses or Special Passes
- **THEN** those passes, the individual tickets and TG01 no longer exist, also after a restart

### Requirement: Common ticket abstraction
Individual and group tickets SHALL be manageable through a common ticket abstraction for identifier lookup, date checks and the users they admit.

#### Scenario: Lookup by identifier
- **WHEN** an operation looks up a ticket identifier
- **THEN** it finds individual or group tickets alike

### Requirement: Group ticket persistence
Group tickets SHALL be stored with identifier, group identifier, date and total price, and their internal tickets with a reference to the group ticket; all SHALL be restored at startup.

#### Scenario: Group ticket survives restart
- **WHEN** TG01 is created for G01, the application exits and starts again
- **THEN** TG01 exists for G01 with the same date, total and individual tickets, and G01 is still locked if TG01 is current or future
