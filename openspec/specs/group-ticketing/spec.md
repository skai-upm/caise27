# group-ticketing Specification

## Purpose

Defines joint ticket purchases by groups: the generated individual tickets, the size-based discount, the frozen composition, the locking of groups while their tickets are valid, refunds and persistence.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Buy group ticket
`GROUPTICKET CREATE;<id>;<groupId>;<yyyy-MM-dd>` SHALL create a group ticket for that date containing one new individual ticket for each user who is a member of the group at that moment, each at the current individual price, and respond `OK GROUP TICKET CREATED -> <total>` with two decimals. It SHALL reject an empty or already used identifier, an unknown group (`ERROR GROUP NOT FOUND`), a group without members (`ERROR EMPTY GROUP`), an invalid date and a date earlier than the system date, with the same messages as individual tickets.

#### Scenario: Two members
- **WHEN** the price is 40.00 and `GROUPTICKET CREATE;TG01;G01;2026-10-15` is processed for a group with 2 members
- **THEN** the response is `OK GROUP TICKET CREATED -> 80.00` and 2 individual tickets dated 2026-10-15 are generated, one per member

#### Scenario: Unknown group
- **WHEN** `GROUPTICKET CREATE;TG02;G99;2026-10-15` is processed
- **THEN** the response is `ERROR GROUP NOT FOUND`

### Requirement: Group discount brackets
The group ticket total SHALL be the sum of its individual ticket prices minus a discount determined by the number of members: fewer than 3 → 0%; 3 to 5 → 10%; 6 to 10 → 20%; more than 10 → 50%.

#### Scenario: Bracket boundaries at 40.00 per ticket
- **WHEN** group tickets are bought for groups of 2, 3, 5, 6, 10 and 11 members
- **THEN** the totals are 80.00, 108.00, 180.00, 192.00, 320.00 and 220.00

### Requirement: Composition frozen at purchase
A group ticket SHALL keep the members it was bought for; adding or removing members afterwards (once allowed) SHALL NOT change its individual tickets.

#### Scenario: Later membership change
- **WHEN** a past group ticket of G01 had Ana and Pedro and later Luis is added to G01
- **THEN** the group ticket still contains exactly the tickets of Ana and Pedro

### Requirement: Group locked while it has valid tickets
While a group has any group ticket dated on or after the system date, `GROUP ADD`, `GROUP REMOVE` and `GROUP DELETE` on it SHALL be rejected with `ERROR GROUP LOCKED`. When all its group tickets are in the past, the group can be modified again.

#### Scenario: Locked group
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01, G01 has group ticket TG01 for 2026-10-15 and `GROUP ADD;G01;luis@correo.com` is processed
- **THEN** the response is `ERROR GROUP LOCKED` and the group is unchanged

#### Scenario: Unlocked after the date
- **WHEN** afterwards `SYSTEM DATE;2026-10-16` is processed and `GROUP ADD;G01;luis@correo.com` is processed
- **THEN** the response is `OK USER ADDED TO GROUP`

### Requirement: Generated tickets protect their users
The individual tickets generated by a group ticket SHALL refer to their users like ordinary individual tickets and SHALL prevent those users from being deleted while dated on or after the system date.

#### Scenario: Member cannot be deleted
- **WHEN** Ana is in group ticket TG01 for 2026-10-15, the system date is 2026-10-01 and `USER DELETE;ana@correo.com;Clave1!` is processed
- **THEN** the response is `ERROR USER HAS ACTIVE TICKETS`

### Requirement: Refund group ticket
`GROUPTICKET DELETE;<id>` SHALL delete the group ticket together with all its internal individual tickets and respond `OK GROUP TICKET DELETED` only when its date is strictly later than the system date; otherwise the response is `ERROR TICKET NOT REFUNDABLE`. An unknown identifier is rejected with `ERROR TICKET NOT FOUND`.

#### Scenario: Future group ticket refunded
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01 and `GROUPTICKET DELETE;TG01` is processed for a group ticket dated 2026-10-15
- **THEN** the response is `OK GROUP TICKET DELETED`, its individual tickets no longer exist and the group is unlocked

### Requirement: Group ticket persistence
Group tickets SHALL be stored in a text file (`data/grouptickets.txt` by default), one per line with identifier, date, group identifier, total and the holder DNI and price of each internal ticket, and restored at startup linked to the restored group and users; past group tickets are kept as history.

#### Scenario: Restored after restart
- **WHEN** a group ticket is bought and the application is restarted
- **THEN** it exists with the same date, group, total and internal tickets, and the group is still locked if the date is current or future
