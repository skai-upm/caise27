# personal-passes Specification

## Purpose

Defines the personal passes attached to individual tickets — SpeedPasses and Special Passes — with their prices, purchase limits, consumption status, refunds and persistence.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Passes belong to individual tickets
Each personal pass SHALL be an independent object with an identifier, a price and a consumption status, associated with exactly one individual ticket — a standalone ticket or one generated by a group ticket. An individual ticket MAY have any number of SpeedPasses and at most one Special Pass. Passes cannot be bought for group tickets as a whole, for unknown tickets (`ERROR TICKET NOT FOUND`) or for tickets dated before the system date (`ERROR TICKET EXPIRED`).

#### Scenario: Pass on a group-generated ticket
- **WHEN** `SPEEDPASS CREATE;TG01-1;1` is processed for the first individual ticket of group ticket TG01
- **THEN** a SpeedPass is attached to ticket TG01-1

#### Scenario: Group ticket itself
- **WHEN** `SPEEDPASS CREATE;TG01;1` is processed
- **THEN** the response is `ERROR TICKET NOT FOUND`

### Requirement: Buy SpeedPasses
`SPEEDPASS CREATE;<ticketId>;<quantity>` SHALL accept quantities 1 and 10 only (`ERROR INVALID QUANTITY` otherwise) and create that many independent SpeedPasses, each priced at 20% of the price stored in the ticket and unconsumed, and respond `OK SPEEDPASS CREATED -> <total> (<first id>..<last id>)` (a single identifier for quantity 1).

#### Scenario: Single SpeedPass
- **WHEN** ticket T001 cost 40.00 and `SPEEDPASS CREATE;T001;1` is processed
- **THEN** the response is `OK SPEEDPASS CREATED -> 8.00 (T001-S1)`

#### Scenario: Pack of ten
- **WHEN** `SPEEDPASS CREATE;T001;10` is then processed
- **THEN** the response is `OK SPEEDPASS CREATED -> 80.00 (T001-S2..T001-S11)` and ten SpeedPasses each priced 8.00 exist

#### Scenario: Other quantity
- **WHEN** `SPEEDPASS CREATE;T001;5` is processed
- **THEN** the response is `ERROR INVALID QUANTITY`

### Requirement: Buy Special Pass
`SPECIALPASS CREATE;<ticketId>` SHALL create the ticket's Special Pass priced at the price stored in the ticket and respond `OK SPECIALPASS CREATED -> <price> (<ticketId>-SPECIAL)`. A ticket that already has a Special Pass, consumed or not, SHALL be rejected with `ERROR SPECIAL PASS ALREADY ISSUED`.

#### Scenario: Only one Special Pass
- **WHEN** `SPECIALPASS CREATE;T001` is processed twice for a ticket that cost 40.00
- **THEN** the responses are `OK SPECIALPASS CREATED -> 40.00 (T001-SPECIAL)` and `ERROR SPECIAL PASS ALREADY ISSUED`

### Requirement: Consumed passes are kept
Consuming a pass SHALL only mark it as consumed; consumed passes remain in the system with that status and keep their price.

#### Scenario: Consumed status
- **WHEN** a SpeedPass is consumed
- **THEN** it still exists, attached to its ticket, and reports consumed

### Requirement: Refund unused passes
`SPEEDPASS DELETE;<passId>` and `SPECIALPASS DELETE;<ticketId>` SHALL remove an unused pass and respond `OK SPEEDPASS DELETED -> <price>` / `OK SPECIALPASS DELETED -> <price>` only when the ticket's date is strictly later than the system date. A consumed pass gives `ERROR PASS CONSUMED`, a ticket dated today or earlier `ERROR TICKET NOT REFUNDABLE`, and an unknown pass `ERROR PASS NOT FOUND`. After refunding its Special Pass a ticket may get a new one.

#### Scenario: Refund before the date
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01, T001 is dated 2026-10-15 and `SPEEDPASS DELETE;T001-S1` is processed for an unused pass
- **THEN** the response is `OK SPEEDPASS DELETED -> 8.00` and the pass no longer exists

#### Scenario: Ticket for today
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-15 and `SPECIALPASS DELETE;T001` is processed
- **THEN** the response is `ERROR TICKET NOT REFUNDABLE`

### Requirement: Pass persistence
Passes SHALL be stored in a text file (`data/passes.txt` by default), one per line as `<ticketId>;<passId>;<SPEED|SPECIAL>;<price>;<consumed>`, rewritten after every pass change and restored onto their tickets at startup; lines whose ticket no longer exists are ignored.

#### Scenario: Restart keeps passes
- **WHEN** passes are bought, one is consumed, and the application restarts
- **THEN** the passes are attached to the same tickets with the same prices and consumption status

### Requirement: Passes follow their ticket
Personal passes SHALL be deleted together with the individual ticket they belong to, both when an individual ticket is refunded and when a group ticket is refunded. If storing the result fails, the deletion SHALL be undone so no ticket or pass is partially deleted, and the response is `ERROR STORAGE FAILURE`.

#### Scenario: Storage failure during refund
- **WHEN** the pass file cannot be written while `TICKET DELETE;T001` is processed for a ticket with passes
- **THEN** the response is `ERROR STORAGE FAILURE` and T001 and its passes still exist

### Requirement: Passes and their state survive restarts
After a restart every pass SHALL be attached to the same individual ticket with the same identifier, kind, price and consumed status it had before.

#### Scenario: Consumed status and price preserved
- **WHEN** a SpeedPass bought at 8.00 is consumed and the application restarts
- **THEN** the pass exists on its ticket with price 8.00 and consumed status
