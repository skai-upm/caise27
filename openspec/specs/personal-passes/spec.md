# personal-passes Specification

## Purpose

Adds personal passes attached to individual tickets: SpeedPasses for priority access and a Special Pass for special attractions, with prices, refunds, consumption state and persistence.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Buy SpeedPasses
The system SHALL accept `SPEEDPASS CREATE;<entrada>;<cantidad>` for an existing individual ticket (direct or generated by a group ticket) dated on or after the system date, with quantity 1 or 10. Each SpeedPass SHALL be an independent object with its own identifier, price (20% of the ticket's stored price, two decimals) and consumption status. The output SHALL show the amount charged and the pass identifiers.

#### Scenario: Buy one
- **WHEN** T001 cost 40.00 and `SPEEDPASS CREATE;T001;1` is processed
- **THEN** the system reports `OK SPEEDPASS CREATED -> 8.00` followed by the new pass identifier

#### Scenario: Buy a pack of ten
- **WHEN** `SPEEDPASS CREATE;T001;10` is processed
- **THEN** ten unused SpeedPasses with distinct identifiers and price 8.00 each exist and the output shows `-> 80.00`

#### Scenario: Invalid purchase
- **WHEN** the quantity is 2, the ticket is a group ticket, the ticket does not exist or its date is before the system date
- **THEN** the system reports an error and no pass is created

### Requirement: Buy Special Pass
The system SHALL accept `SPECIALPASS CREATE;<entrada>` for an existing individual ticket dated on or after the system date, priced at the ticket's stored price, and SHALL reject it while the ticket already has a Special Pass, consumed or not.

#### Scenario: Buy special pass
- **WHEN** T001 cost 40.00 and `SPECIALPASS CREATE;T001` is processed
- **THEN** the system reports `OK SPECIALPASS CREATED -> 40.00` followed by the pass identifier

#### Scenario: Second special pass
- **WHEN** T001 already has a Special Pass (even a consumed one) and `SPECIALPASS CREATE;T001` is processed
- **THEN** the system reports an error

### Requirement: Refund passes
The system SHALL accept `SPEEDPASS DELETE;<pase>` and `SPECIALPASS DELETE;<pase>` and delete an unused pass of the matching kind when its ticket's date is strictly later than the system date, reporting the refunded amount. Consumed passes, passes of tickets dated today or earlier, and passes of the other kind SHALL be rejected.

#### Scenario: Refund unused pass
- **WHEN** the system date is before T001's date and `SPEEDPASS DELETE;<id>` names an unused SpeedPass of T001
- **THEN** the system reports `OK SPEEDPASS DELETED -> 8.00` and the pass no longer exists

#### Scenario: Consumed pass
- **WHEN** a consumed pass is named
- **THEN** the system reports an error and the pass remains

### Requirement: Consumed passes are kept
Using a pass SHALL mark it as consumed without removing it.

#### Scenario: Consumed pass remains
- **WHEN** a SpeedPass is used to enter an attraction
- **THEN** the pass still exists and is marked consumed

### Requirement: Pass persistence
Passes SHALL be stored with identifier, kind, associated individual ticket, price and consumption status and restored at startup, also in `--fly` mode.

#### Scenario: Passes survive restart
- **WHEN** T001 has a consumed SpeedPass and an unused Special Pass and the application restarts
- **THEN** both passes exist with the same ticket, price and consumption status

### Requirement: Cascading deletion
When a future individual ticket is deleted, its SpeedPasses and Special Pass SHALL be deleted with it. When a future group ticket is deleted, the passes of each generated ticket SHALL be deleted, then those tickets, then the group ticket. A rejected deletion SHALL leave everything unchanged.

#### Scenario: Ticket deletion removes passes
- **WHEN** T001 is future, has passes and `TICKET DELETE;T001` is processed
- **THEN** T001 and all its passes no longer exist

#### Scenario: Group ticket deletion removes passes
- **WHEN** TG01 is future, TG01-1 has a SpeedPass and `GROUPTICKET DELETE;TG01` is processed
- **THEN** TG01, its generated tickets and their passes no longer exist

#### Scenario: Failed deletion changes nothing
- **WHEN** `TICKET DELETE;T001` is rejected because T001 is dated today
- **THEN** T001 and its passes still exist
