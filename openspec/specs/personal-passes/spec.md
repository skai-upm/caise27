# personal-passes Specification

## Purpose

Sells personal SpeedPasses and Special Passes attached to individual tickets, prices them from the ticket, allows refunds of unused passes, and keeps them persistent.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Buy SpeedPasses
`SPEEDPASS CREATE;<entrada>;<cantidad>` SHALL accept only quantities 1 and 10 for an existing individual ticket (including tickets generated by group tickets) dated today or later, creating that many independent SpeedPasses, each with its own identifier, price (20 % of the ticket's stored price) and unconsumed status. The reply SHALL show the amount charged.

#### Scenario: Pack of ten
- **WHEN** `SPEEDPASS CREATE;T001;10` is executed for T001 priced 40.00
- **THEN** ten SpeedPasses priced 8.00 each are attached to T001 and the reply shows `-> 80.00`

#### Scenario: Invalid quantity
- **WHEN** `SPEEDPASS CREATE;T001;5` is executed
- **THEN** an error is reported and no pass is created

#### Scenario: Group ticket id
- **WHEN** `SPEEDPASS CREATE;TG01;1` is executed with a group ticket id
- **THEN** an error is reported

### Requirement: Buy Special Pass
`SPECIALPASS CREATE;<entrada>` SHALL create a Special Pass priced like the ticket's stored price for an existing individual ticket dated today or later, replying with the amount charged. An individual ticket SHALL NOT have more than one Special Pass, even if it has been consumed.

#### Scenario: Buy
- **WHEN** `SPECIALPASS CREATE;T001` is executed for T001 priced 40.00
- **THEN** a Special Pass priced 40.00 is attached and the reply shows `-> 40.00`

#### Scenario: Second Special Pass
- **WHEN** T001 already has a Special Pass (consumed or not) and `SPECIALPASS CREATE;T001` is executed
- **THEN** an error is reported

### Requirement: Refund unused passes
`SPEEDPASS DELETE;<idPase>` and `SPECIALPASS DELETE;<idPase>` SHALL remove and refund an unconsumed pass only when its ticket's date is strictly later than the current date, replying with the refunded amount. Consumed passes SHALL NOT be refunded and SHALL remain stored with their consumed status.

#### Scenario: Refund
- **WHEN** the date is 2026-10-01 and an unused SpeedPass of a 2026-10-15 ticket is deleted
- **THEN** it is removed and the reply shows the refunded amount

#### Scenario: Consumed or today
- **WHEN** the pass is consumed, or the ticket is dated today
- **THEN** the refund is rejected and the pass remains

### Requirement: Pass persistence
Passes SHALL be stored in a text file with identifier, type, associated individual ticket, price and consumed status, and restored at startup attached to their tickets. `--fly` runs SHALL use the temporary directory for this file as well.

#### Scenario: Restart
- **WHEN** passes are bought, one is consumed, and the application restarts
- **THEN** the passes exist with the same prices, tickets and consumed status
