# ticket-management Specification

## Purpose

Lets registered users buy individual tickets for a date, with persistent history and date-based deletion rules.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Buy individual ticket
`TICKET CREATE;id;email;yyyy-MM-dd` SHALL create a ticket with a unique identifier, the date of use, a reference to the existing user and the current ticket price, and SHALL answer `OK TICKET CREATED -> <price>`. Ticket identifiers SHALL be unique across individual and group tickets.

#### Scenario: Buy ticket
- **WHEN** the price is 40.00 and `TICKET CREATE;T001;ana@correo.com;2026-10-15` is processed with the system date 2026-10-01
- **THEN** the answer is `OK TICKET CREATED -> 40.00` and T001 belongs to Ana for 2026-10-15

#### Scenario: Unknown user
- **WHEN** `TICKET CREATE;T002;nadie@correo.com;2026-10-15` is processed
- **THEN** an `ERROR` is reported and no ticket is created

#### Scenario: Duplicate identifier
- **WHEN** T001 exists and `TICKET CREATE;T001;pedro@correo.com;2026-10-15` is processed
- **THEN** an `ERROR` is reported

#### Scenario: Date before the system date
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01 and `TICKET CREATE;T003;ana@correo.com;2026-09-30` is processed
- **THEN** an `ERROR` is reported because tickets cannot be bought for past dates

### Requirement: Delete future ticket
`TICKET DELETE;id` SHALL delete an individual ticket only when its date is strictly later than the system date, deleting all its SpeedPasses and its Special Pass with it. Tickets that are part of a group ticket SHALL NOT be deleted individually.

#### Scenario: Future ticket deleted
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01 and `TICKET DELETE;T001` is processed for a ticket dated 2026-10-15
- **THEN** T001 is deleted

#### Scenario: Today's ticket kept
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-15 and `TICKET DELETE;T001` is processed for a ticket dated 2026-10-15
- **THEN** an `ERROR` is reported and T001 remains

#### Scenario: Internal ticket of a group ticket
- **WHEN** `TICKET DELETE` names an individual ticket generated by a group ticket
- **THEN** an `ERROR` is reported and the ticket remains

#### Scenario: Passes deleted with the ticket
- **WHEN** future ticket T001 has SpeedPasses and a Special Pass and `TICKET DELETE;T001` is processed
- **THEN** T001 and all its passes are deleted, also from storage

### Requirement: Tickets persist as history
Individual tickets, including those whose date has passed, SHALL be stored in a `;`-separated text file and restored with their identifier, date, price and user when the application starts again.

#### Scenario: Restart restores tickets
- **WHEN** T001 exists with price 60.00 and the application restarts
- **THEN** T001 exists with the same date, user and price 60.00, even though the current price is back to 40.00
