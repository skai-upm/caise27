# ticket-management Specification

## Purpose

Lets registered users buy individual tickets for a date of use, keeps every ticket (including past ones) persistently, and manages the current ticket price.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Ticket data
Each ticket SHALL have an identifier, a date of use, the registered user it belongs to (a reference to that user, not a copy) and the price paid.

#### Scenario: Ticket fields
- **WHEN** ticket `T001` is sold to `ana@correo.com` for 2026-10-15 at price 40.00
- **THEN** the ticket has identifier `T001`, date 2026-10-15, user Ana and price 40.00

### Requirement: Create individual ticket
The command `TICKET CREATE;Id;Correo;yyyy-MM-dd` SHALL create a ticket for the registered user with that email, for that date, at the current ticket price, and SHALL answer exactly `OK TICKET CREATED -> <Precio>` with the price formatted with two decimals. The identifier SHALL be non-empty and not used by any existing ticket, the user SHALL exist, and the date SHALL be a valid `yyyy-MM-dd` date not earlier than the system date; otherwise an error SHALL be reported and no ticket created.

#### Scenario: Ticket created
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01, user `ana@correo.com` exists and `TICKET CREATE;T001;ana@correo.com;2026-10-15` is received
- **THEN** ticket `T001` is stored and `OK TICKET CREATED -> 40.00` is printed

#### Scenario: Nonexistent user
- **WHEN** `TICKET CREATE;T002;nadie@correo.com;2026-10-15` is received
- **THEN** an error is printed and no ticket is created

#### Scenario: Duplicate identifier
- **WHEN** ticket `T001` exists and `TICKET CREATE;T001;ana@correo.com;2026-10-20` is received
- **THEN** an error is printed and ticket `T001` is unchanged

#### Scenario: Invalid or past date
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01 and the date parameter is `2026-13-01` or `2026-09-30`
- **THEN** an error is printed and no ticket is created

### Requirement: Current ticket price
The application SHALL keep a current ticket price that starts at `40.00` on every execution and is not persisted. The command `SYSTEM PRICE;<importe>` SHALL change it to a positive decimal amount with at most two decimals; other values SHALL be rejected leaving the price unchanged. Already sold tickets SHALL keep the price they were sold at.

#### Scenario: Price changed
- **WHEN** `SYSTEM PRICE;60.00` is received and then `TICKET CREATE;T003;ana@correo.com;2026-10-20`
- **THEN** the response is `OK TICKET CREATED -> 60.00`

#### Scenario: Invalid price
- **WHEN** any of `SYSTEM PRICE;-5`, `SYSTEM PRICE;0`, `SYSTEM PRICE;abc`, `SYSTEM PRICE;10.555` is received
- **THEN** an invalid-price error is printed and the current price is unchanged

#### Scenario: Price resets on restart
- **WHEN** the price was changed to 60.00 and the application is restarted
- **THEN** the current price is 40.00 and tickets sold before keep their stored price

### Requirement: Delete ticket
The command `TICKET DELETE;Id` SHALL delete an individual ticket only if it exists, is not part of a group ticket, and its date is strictly later than the system date; a ticket dated on or before the system date SHALL NOT be deleted and an error SHALL be reported. Individual tickets generated by a group ticket SHALL only be deleted through `GROUPTICKET DELETE`.

#### Scenario: Future ticket deleted
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01 and ticket `T001` is dated 2026-10-15 and `TICKET DELETE;T001` is received
- **THEN** ticket `T001` is deleted

#### Scenario: Ticket for today
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-15 and ticket `T001` is dated 2026-10-15 and `TICKET DELETE;T001` is received
- **THEN** an error is printed and the ticket is kept

#### Scenario: Unknown ticket
- **WHEN** `TICKET DELETE;T999` is received and no such ticket exists
- **THEN** an error is printed

#### Scenario: Internal ticket of a group ticket
- **WHEN** group ticket `TG01` generated internal ticket `TG01-1` and `TICKET DELETE;TG01-1` is received
- **THEN** an error indicating that the ticket belongs to group ticket `TG01` is printed and nothing is deleted

### Requirement: Ticket persistence and history
All tickets, including those whose date is already past, SHALL be stored in a text file and restored when the application starts, linked to the registered user they belong to. Past tickets SHALL be retained as historical records, including when their user has been deleted.

#### Scenario: Restart restores tickets
- **WHEN** ticket `T001` is created, the application is restarted
- **THEN** ticket `T001` exists with its date, price and the restored user Ana

#### Scenario: History kept after user deletion
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-16, Ana's only ticket `T001` is dated 2026-10-15 and Ana is deleted
- **THEN** ticket `T001` is still stored as a historical record

### Requirement: Create group ticket
The command `GROUPTICKET CREATE;Id;IdGrupo;yyyy-MM-dd` SHALL create a group ticket for an existing group with at least one member, for a valid date not earlier than the system date, with an identifier not used by any ticket. It SHALL generate one individual ticket per member of the group at that moment, each referencing its user, dated on the group ticket date and priced at the current individual price; the internal tickets SHALL be identified `<Id>-1`, `<Id>-2`, … in member order. It SHALL answer exactly `OK GROUP TICKET CREATED -> <Precio>` with the discounted total formatted with two decimals. On any failure an error SHALL be reported and nothing created.

#### Scenario: Group ticket created
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01, group `G01` has members Ana and Pedro and `GROUPTICKET CREATE;TG01;G01;2026-10-15` is received with price 40.00
- **THEN** group ticket `TG01` with internal tickets `TG01-1` (Ana) and `TG01-2` (Pedro) dated 2026-10-15 is created and `OK GROUP TICKET CREATED -> 80.00` is printed

#### Scenario: Unknown or empty group
- **WHEN** the group does not exist or has no members
- **THEN** an error is printed and no ticket is created

#### Scenario: Duplicate identifier
- **WHEN** any ticket with identifier `TG01`, or an identifier equal to one of the internal identifiers to generate, already exists
- **THEN** an error is printed and no ticket is created

#### Scenario: Invalid or past date
- **WHEN** the date is not a valid `yyyy-MM-dd` date or is earlier than the system date
- **THEN** an error is printed and no ticket is created

### Requirement: Group discount
The group ticket total SHALL be the sum of its internal individual ticket prices minus a discount determined by the number of members: fewer than 3 → 0%, 3 to 5 → 10%, 6 to 10 → 20%, more than 10 → 50%. The total SHALL be rounded to two decimals.

#### Scenario: Discount brackets with price 40.00
- **WHEN** group tickets are bought for groups of 2, 3, 5, 6, 10 and 11 members at price 40.00
- **THEN** the totals are 80.00, 108.00, 180.00, 192.00, 320.00 and 220.00 respectively

### Requirement: Group composition fixed at purchase
A group ticket SHALL keep the members it was bought for; later changes to the group SHALL NOT add or remove internal tickets.

#### Scenario: Member added after the ticket date
- **WHEN** group ticket `TG01` for 2026-10-15 covered Ana and Pedro, the system date moves to 2026-10-16 and Luis is added to the group
- **THEN** `TG01` still has exactly the internal tickets for Ana and Pedro

### Requirement: Internal tickets protect their users
Internal individual tickets of a group ticket SHALL count as tickets of their users, so a user with an internal ticket dated on or after the system date SHALL NOT be deleted.

#### Scenario: Member with a current group ticket
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01, Ana is covered by group ticket `TG01` for 2026-10-15 and `USER DELETE;ana@correo.com;Clave1!` is received
- **THEN** the deletion is rejected

#### Scenario: Restriction lifted as the date advances
- **WHEN** the system date becomes 2026-10-16 and Ana has no other current or future ticket
- **THEN** `USER DELETE;ana@correo.com;Clave1!` succeeds and `TG01` is kept as history

### Requirement: Delete group ticket
The command `GROUPTICKET DELETE;Id` SHALL delete an existing group ticket together with all its internal individual tickets only if its date is strictly later than the system date; otherwise an error SHALL be reported and nothing deleted.

#### Scenario: Future group ticket deleted
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01 and `GROUPTICKET DELETE;TG01` is received for a ticket dated 2026-10-15
- **THEN** `TG01`, `TG01-1` and `TG01-2` no longer exist

#### Scenario: Group ticket for today or past
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-15 and `GROUPTICKET DELETE;TG01` is received
- **THEN** an error is printed and the tickets are kept

#### Scenario: Unknown group ticket
- **WHEN** `GROUPTICKET DELETE;TG99` is received and no group ticket has that identifier
- **THEN** an error is printed

### Requirement: Group ticket persistence
Group tickets, with their date, group, base price, discount and internal tickets (identifier and user), SHALL be stored in a text file and restored at startup linked to the restored group and users; past group tickets SHALL be kept as history even if their group or users were deleted.

#### Scenario: Restart restores group tickets
- **WHEN** group ticket `TG01` is created and the application is restarted
- **THEN** `TG01` exists with the same total, group and internal tickets referencing the restored users
