# invoicing Specification

## Purpose

Generates on-demand invoices for users and groups from the purchases recorded in the system, assembled step by step with a Builder.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Invoice commands
The system SHALL accept `INVOICE CREATE;USER;<correo>` for an existing user and `INVOICE CREATE;GROUP;<grupo>` for an existing group and display the generated invoice. Unknown recipients or recipient types SHALL be rejected. Invoices SHALL NOT be persisted.

#### Scenario: Unknown recipient
- **WHEN** `INVOICE CREATE;USER;nadie@correo.com` or `INVOICE CREATE;GROUP;G99` or `INVOICE CREATE;SHOP;X` is processed
- **THEN** the system reports an error

### Requirement: Builder assembly
Invoices SHALL be assembled through a Builder abstraction in distinct steps (recipient, then each applicable section, then the result), without constructors taking many optional parameters for invoice variants. Only sections applicable to the recipient type and with available data SHALL be included.

#### Scenario: Empty sections omitted
- **WHEN** an invoice is generated for a user without passes
- **THEN** the invoice has no personal passes section

### Requirement: User invoice content
A user invoice SHALL list the individual tickets bought directly for that user and the personal passes of any of the user's individual tickets, including tickets generated by group tickets. Tickets generated by a group ticket SHALL NOT be listed as individual purchases.

#### Scenario: User with direct and group tickets
- **WHEN** Ana bought T001 (40.00) and a SpeedPass for it (8.00), and is in group ticket TG01 whose generated ticket TG01-1 has a Special Pass (40.00)
- **THEN** her invoice lists T001 under individual tickets, both passes under personal passes, does not list TG01-1 as an individual ticket, and its total is 88.00

### Requirement: Group invoice content
A group invoice SHALL list the group's group-ticket purchases with their prices and SHALL NOT add their generated individual tickets or the members' personal passes.

#### Scenario: Group invoice
- **WHEN** group G01 bought TG01 for 108.00 and members have passes
- **THEN** the G01 invoice lists TG01 with 108.00 and its total is 108.00

### Requirement: Prices and total
Each invoice line SHALL show the item's price with two decimals and the invoice SHALL show the total of all lines.

#### Scenario: Total shown
- **WHEN** any invoice is generated
- **THEN** it ends with a `TOTAL` line equal to the sum of its lines
