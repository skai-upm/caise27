# invoicing Specification

## Purpose

Generates on-demand invoices for users and groups from the purchases recorded in the system, assembling only the sections that apply to each recipient.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Invoice construction with Builder
Invoices SHALL be assembled step by step through a builder abstraction (one step per section plus a final build step), driven by a director that chooses the steps for the recipient type. Invoice variants SHALL NOT be produced through constructors with many optional parameters. Invoices SHALL NOT be persisted.

#### Scenario: Builder steps
- **WHEN** an invoice is generated
- **THEN** it is produced by calling the builder's section steps and then its build step

### Requirement: User invoice
`INVOICE CREATE;USER;<email>` SHALL show, for a registered user, a section with the individual tickets bought directly for that user, and sections with the SpeedPasses and Special Passes attached to any of the user's individual tickets (including tickets generated by a group ticket). Tickets generated by a group ticket SHALL NOT be listed as individual purchases. Unknown users SHALL be rejected.

#### Scenario: Tickets and passes
- **WHEN** Ana has ticket T001 (40.00) with one SpeedPass (8.00), is a member of a group ticket TG01 whose generated ticket has a Special Pass (40.00), and `INVOICE CREATE;USER;ana@correo.com` is executed
- **THEN** the invoice lists T001 at 40.00, the SpeedPass at 8.00 and the Special Pass at 40.00, does not list TG01's generated ticket, and shows a total of 88.00

### Requirement: Group invoice
`INVOICE CREATE;GROUP;<groupId>` SHALL show, for an existing group, a section with its group tickets and their amounts, without the generated individual tickets or members' passes. Unknown groups SHALL be rejected.

#### Scenario: Group tickets only
- **WHEN** group G01 bought TG01 (108.00) and `INVOICE CREATE;GROUP;G01` is executed
- **THEN** the invoice lists TG01 at 108.00 and a total of 108.00, and no member tickets or passes

### Requirement: Applicable sections and totals
An invoice SHALL include only sections for which the recipient has items, SHALL show each item's price, and SHALL show the total of all items. A recipient without items gets an invoice with no sections and a total of 0.00.

#### Scenario: No purchases
- **WHEN** a user with no tickets requests an invoice
- **THEN** the invoice has no item sections and its total is 0.00
