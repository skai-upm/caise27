# invoicing Specification

## Purpose

Defines on-demand invoices for users and groups: which purchases each one includes, how prices and totals are shown, and that invoices are assembled step by step through a builder.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Invoice commands
`INVOICE CREATE;USER;<email>` SHALL print the invoice of the registered user with that email and `INVOICE CREATE;GROUP;<groupId>` the invoice of that group. An unknown user gives `ERROR USER NOT FOUND`, an unknown group `ERROR GROUP NOT FOUND`, and any recipient type other than `USER` or `GROUP` `ERROR INVALID INVOICE TYPE`. Invoices are generated on demand and are not persisted.

#### Scenario: Unknown recipient type
- **WHEN** `INVOICE CREATE;FAMILY;G01` is processed
- **THEN** the response is `ERROR INVALID INVOICE TYPE`

### Requirement: Invoice layout
An invoice SHALL start with the line `INVOICE <USER|GROUP> <identifier> - <name>` followed by `Date: <system date>`, then one block per non-empty section (a title line and one line per item `- <id> <date> ... <price>`), and end with `TOTAL: <amount>` with two decimals. When no section has items it shows `No purchases` and `TOTAL: 0.00`.

#### Scenario: Invoice without purchases
- **WHEN** a user with no tickets requests an invoice on 2026-10-01
- **THEN** the output is `INVOICE USER ana@correo.com - Ana Garcia Lopez`, `Date: 2026-10-01`, `No purchases`, `TOTAL: 0.00`

### Requirement: User invoice content
A user invoice SHALL include, in an `Individual tickets` section, every individual ticket bought directly for that user with its price, and the total of those prices. Individual tickets generated by a group ticket SHALL NOT be counted on the user's invoice.

#### Scenario: Direct and group tickets
- **WHEN** Ana bought T001 at 40.00 and T002 at 60.00 and is also a member of group ticket TG01
- **THEN** her invoice lists T001 (40.00) and T002 (60.00), does not list TG01 or its internal tickets, and shows `TOTAL: 100.00`

### Requirement: Group invoice content
A group invoice SHALL include, in a `Group tickets` section, every group ticket bought by that group with its member count, discount and discounted amount, and the total of those amounts, without adding the composing individual tickets again.

#### Scenario: Group with one group ticket
- **WHEN** group G01 with 3 members bought TG01 at 40.00 per member
- **THEN** its invoice lists `- TG01 2026-10-15 (3 members, 10% discount) 108.00` and `TOTAL: 108.00`

### Requirement: Invoices built with a builder
Invoices SHALL be constructed through a builder abstraction that assembles them in distinct steps (recipient, date, each section and its lines) and computes the total when the invoice is built; only sections with data are included, and no constructor with numerous optional parameters is used for invoice variants.

#### Scenario: Empty section omitted
- **WHEN** a builder receives a section with no lines and then builds the invoice
- **THEN** the resulting invoice has no such section
