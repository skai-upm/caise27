# ticketing Specification

## Purpose

Lets registered users buy individual tickets for a date at a configurable price, delete future tickets, and keeps all tickets persistently as history.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Ticket data
Every ticket SHALL have an identifier, a date of use and a price. An individual ticket SHALL hold a reference to the user it belongs to. Ticket identifiers SHALL be unique across individual and group tickets.

#### Scenario: Ticket contents
- **WHEN** `TICKET CREATE;T001;ana@correo.com;2026-10-15` succeeds with price 40.00
- **THEN** ticket T001 has date 2026-10-15, belongs to Ana and has price 40.00

### Requirement: Ticket price setting
The application SHALL keep a current individual ticket price, initialised to 40.00 on each execution and not persisted. `SYSTEM PRICE;amount` SHALL set it to a positive decimal amount and print `OK SYSTEM PRICE -> <amount>`. Prices SHALL be shown with two decimals.

#### Scenario: Change price
- **WHEN** `SYSTEM PRICE;60.00` is processed and then a ticket is created
- **THEN** the new ticket costs 60.00

#### Scenario: Invalid price
- **WHEN** `SYSTEM PRICE;-5`, `SYSTEM PRICE;0` or `SYSTEM PRICE;abc` is processed
- **THEN** an `ERROR` result is printed and the price is unchanged

### Requirement: Create individual ticket
`TICKET CREATE;id;email;yyyy-MM-dd` SHALL create a ticket for the existing user with that email, for a date not earlier than the system date, at the current price, and print `OK TICKET CREATED -> <price>`.

#### Scenario: Successful purchase
- **WHEN** `TICKET CREATE;T001;ana@correo.com;2026-10-15` is processed with system date 2026-10-01 and price 40.00
- **THEN** the ticket is created and `OK TICKET CREATED -> 40.00` is printed

#### Scenario: Unknown user
- **WHEN** `TICKET CREATE;T002;nadie@correo.com;2026-10-15` is processed
- **THEN** an `ERROR` result is printed and no ticket is created

#### Scenario: Duplicate identifier or invalid date
- **WHEN** the identifier is already used by any ticket, or the date is malformed or earlier than the system date
- **THEN** an `ERROR` result is printed and no ticket is created

### Requirement: Delete individual ticket
`TICKET DELETE;id` SHALL delete an individual ticket only when its date is later than the system date (the same day does not count). Tickets that are part of a group ticket SHALL NOT be deleted with this command.

#### Scenario: Future ticket deleted
- **WHEN** ticket T001 dated 2026-10-15 exists and the system date is 2026-10-14
- **THEN** `TICKET DELETE;T001` deletes it and prints `OK TICKET DELETED`

#### Scenario: Ticket for today or the past
- **WHEN** ticket T001 dated 2026-10-15 exists and the system date is 2026-10-15 or later
- **THEN** `TICKET DELETE;T001` prints an `ERROR` result and the ticket remains

#### Scenario: Internal ticket of a group ticket
- **WHEN** `TICKET DELETE` targets an individual ticket generated by a group ticket
- **THEN** an `ERROR` result is printed and the ticket remains

### Requirement: Ticket history and persistence
Tickets SHALL be stored in a semicolon-separated text file with their identifier, date, owner DNI and price, and restored at startup linked to their users. Past tickets SHALL be retained as historical records, also when their user has later been deleted.

#### Scenario: Tickets survive restart
- **WHEN** ticket T001 is created, the application exits and starts again
- **THEN** T001 exists with the same date, user and price

#### Scenario: Past tickets are kept
- **WHEN** the system date moves past a ticket's date
- **THEN** the ticket is still stored
