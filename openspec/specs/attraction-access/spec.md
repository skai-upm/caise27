# attraction-access Specification

## Purpose

Defines how visitors enter and leave attractions with individual or group tickets, the date and capacity rules that apply, and how the current status of an attraction is shown.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Uniform ticket-based entry
The system SHALL process `ATTRACTION ENTER;IdAtraccion;IdTicket` for individual tickets, group tickets and the individual tickets generated by a group ticket, through one common entry procedure. An individual ticket SHALL admit its user, and a group ticket SHALL admit the users of all its constituent individual tickets.

#### Scenario: Individual entry
- **GIVEN** the system date is 2026-10-15, `A01` has free places and `T001` is Ana's ticket for 2026-10-15
- **WHEN** `ATTRACTION ENTER;A01;T001` is processed
- **THEN** the result starts with `OK` and Ana is present in `A01`

#### Scenario: Group entry
- **GIVEN** the system date is 2026-10-15 and `TG01` for 2026-10-15 contains tickets for Pedro, Luis and Eva
- **WHEN** `ATTRACTION ENTER;A01;TG01` is processed with at least three free places
- **THEN** the result starts with `OK` and Pedro, Luis and Eva are present

### Requirement: Ticket valid only on its date
Entry SHALL be admitted only if the ticket's date equals the current system date. The date SHALL NOT be checked on exit.

#### Scenario: Ticket for another day
- **GIVEN** `T001` is dated 2026-10-15
- **WHEN** `ATTRACTION ENTER;A01;T001` is processed with the system date 2026-10-14 or 2026-10-16
- **THEN** the result starts with `ERROR` and nobody enters

### Requirement: Capacity and atomic entry
An entry SHALL be rejected as a whole, admitting nobody, if the number of users it would admit exceeds the attraction's available places, if any of those users is already present in that attraction, or if the ticket has no existing users. The number of users present SHALL never exceed the maximum capacity.

#### Scenario: Group larger than the free places
- **GIVEN** `A01` has three available places and `TG01` represents four users
- **WHEN** `ATTRACTION ENTER;A01;TG01` is processed
- **THEN** the result starts with `ERROR` and none of the four users enters

#### Scenario: Exactly the free places
- **GIVEN** `A01` has three available places and `TG01` represents three users
- **WHEN** `ATTRACTION ENTER;A01;TG01` is processed
- **THEN** all three enter and zero places remain

#### Scenario: Already present
- **WHEN** `ATTRACTION ENTER;A01;T001` is processed twice
- **THEN** the second entry is rejected with `ERROR` and Ana is counted once

#### Scenario: Unknown ids or wrong parameters
- **WHEN** the attraction or ticket does not exist, or the command does not have exactly two parameters
- **THEN** the result starts with `ERROR` and nobody enters

### Requirement: Exit with a ticket
The system SHALL process `ATTRACTION EXIT;IdAtraccion;IdTicket`, removing from the attraction every user of that ticket who is present and freeing their places. It SHALL be rejected with `ERROR` if the attraction or ticket does not exist or none of the ticket's users is present.

#### Scenario: Exit frees capacity
- **GIVEN** Ana entered `A01` (capacity 20) with `T001` and the group of `TG01` (3 users) also entered
- **WHEN** `ATTRACTION EXIT;A01;T001` is processed and then `ATTRACTION STATUS;A01`
- **THEN** the exit starts with `OK` and the status shows 3 users present and 17 available places

#### Scenario: Exit on a later date
- **GIVEN** Ana entered `A01` with `T001` on 2026-10-15
- **WHEN** `SYSTEM DATE;2026-10-16` and then `ATTRACTION EXIT;A01;T001` are processed
- **THEN** the exit succeeds

#### Scenario: Nobody of that ticket inside
- **WHEN** `ATTRACTION EXIT;A01;T001` is processed and Ana is not present in `A01`
- **THEN** the result starts with `ERROR`

### Requirement: Attraction status
The system SHALL process `ATTRACTION STATUS;IdAtraccion`, printing a first line starting with `OK` with the attraction's identifier, name, maximum capacity, number of users present and number of available places, followed by one line per user present. Users SHALL be listed the same way however they entered. Unknown attractions SHALL be rejected with `ERROR`.

#### Scenario: Status after mixed entries
- **GIVEN** `A01` (Tornado, capacity 20) after `ATTRACTION ENTER;A01;T001` (Ana) and `ATTRACTION ENTER;A01;TG01` (Pedro, Luis, Eva)
- **WHEN** `ATTRACTION STATUS;A01` is processed
- **THEN** the output shows `A01`, `Tornado`, capacity 20, 4 present, 16 available, and lists Ana, Pedro, Luis and Eva

### Requirement: Deleted users leave attractions
When a user is deleted, the system SHALL remove that user from every attraction they are present in.

#### Scenario: Deleted visitor
- **GIVEN** Ana is present in `A01` and her ticket's date has passed
- **WHEN** `USER DELETE;ana@correo.com;Clave1!` succeeds
- **THEN** `ATTRACTION STATUS;A01` no longer lists her and one more place is available
