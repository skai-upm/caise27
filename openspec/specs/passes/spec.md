# passes Specification

## Purpose

Adds personal SpeedPasses and Special Passes attached to individual tickets, with their own prices, consumption status, purchase and refund rules.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Pass data
Each pass SHALL be a SpeedPass or a Special Pass with an identifier, the price charged, a reference to the individual ticket it belongs to (a ticket bought with `TICKET CREATE` or one generated by a group ticket), and a consumed status that starts as not consumed. Pass identifiers SHALL be unique among all passes. Consumed passes SHALL remain recorded with their status.

#### Scenario: New pass state
- **WHEN** a pass is bought for ticket `T001`
- **THEN** it records its identifier, its price, `T001` as its ticket and the status not consumed

### Requirement: Buy SpeedPasses
The system SHALL process `SPEEDPASS CREATE;IdCompra;IdTicket;Cantidad`, where `Cantidad` is exactly `1` or `10`, creating that many independent SpeedPasses with identifiers `IdCompra-1` to `IdCompra-<Cantidad>` for the individual ticket `IdTicket`, which must exist and be dated on or after the system date. Each SpeedPass SHALL cost 20% of the price stored in that ticket (two decimals, half up), and the command SHALL print `OK SPEEDPASS CREATED -> <total>`, the total being the sum of the passes' prices with no further discount. If any check fails or any generated identifier is already used, nothing SHALL be created and the result SHALL start with `ERROR`.

#### Scenario: Pack of ten
- **GIVEN** ticket `T001` (price 40.00) is dated on or after the system date
- **WHEN** `SPEEDPASS CREATE;SP1;T001;10` is processed
- **THEN** it prints `OK SPEEDPASS CREATED -> 80.00` and passes `SP1-1` to `SP1-10` exist, each priced 8.00 and not consumed

#### Scenario: Single pass
- **WHEN** `SPEEDPASS CREATE;SP2;T001;1` is processed
- **THEN** it prints `OK SPEEDPASS CREATED -> 8.00` and pass `SP2-1` exists

#### Scenario: Disallowed quantity
- **WHEN** `SPEEDPASS CREATE;SP3;T001;5`, `...;0`, `...;11` or `...;diez` is processed
- **THEN** the result starts with `ERROR` and no pass is created

#### Scenario: Identifier conflict
- **GIVEN** pass `SP1-3` already exists
- **WHEN** `SPEEDPASS CREATE;SP1;T001;10` is processed again
- **THEN** the result starts with `ERROR` and no new pass is created

#### Scenario: Unknown or past ticket
- **WHEN** the ticket does not exist or is dated before the system date
- **THEN** the result starts with `ERROR` and no pass is created

### Requirement: Buy a Special Pass
The system SHALL process `SPECIALPASS CREATE;IdPase;IdTicket`, creating a Special Pass that costs exactly the price stored in the individual ticket, only if the ticket exists, is dated on or after the system date, the identifier is unused, and the ticket has no Special Pass recorded (consumed or not). It SHALL print `OK SPECIALPASS CREATED -> <precio>`. Otherwise it SHALL print a line starting with `ERROR` and create nothing.

#### Scenario: Buy a Special Pass
- **WHEN** `SPECIALPASS CREATE;VIP1;T001` is processed for `T001` priced 40.00 and dated on or after the system date
- **THEN** it prints `OK SPECIALPASS CREATED -> 40.00`

#### Scenario: Second Special Pass for the same ticket
- **GIVEN** `T001` already has a Special Pass, consumed or not
- **WHEN** `SPECIALPASS CREATE;VIP2;T001` is processed
- **THEN** the result starts with `ERROR` and no pass is created

### Requirement: Pass price from the stored ticket price
Pass prices SHALL be computed from the price stored in the associated individual ticket at purchase time. For a ticket generated by a group ticket, that stored price is the discounted per-member price.

#### Scenario: Pass on a discounted group-ticket member
- **GIVEN** group ticket `TG01` for 3 members at 40.00 (10% discount) generated `TG01-12345678Z` with stored price 36.00
- **WHEN** `SPEEDPASS CREATE;SP4;TG01-12345678Z;1` and `SPECIALPASS CREATE;VIP3;TG01-12345678Z` are processed
- **THEN** they print `OK SPEEDPASS CREATED -> 7.20` and `OK SPECIALPASS CREATED -> 36.00`

### Requirement: Refund passes
The system SHALL process `SPEEDPASS DELETE;IdPase` and `SPECIALPASS DELETE;IdPase`, removing (refunding) the pass only if it exists, is of the kind named by the command, is not consumed, and its ticket is dated later than the system date. Otherwise it SHALL print a line starting with `ERROR` and keep the pass.

#### Scenario: Refund an unused future pass
- **GIVEN** `SP1-1` is not consumed and `T001` is dated after the system date
- **WHEN** `SPEEDPASS DELETE;SP1-1` is processed
- **THEN** the result starts with `OK` and `SP1-1` no longer exists

#### Scenario: Refund not permitted
- **WHEN** the pass is consumed, its ticket is dated on or before the system date, the pass does not exist, or `SPECIALPASS DELETE` names a SpeedPass (or the reverse)
- **THEN** the result starts with `ERROR` and the pass is kept
