# passes Specification

## Purpose

Lets users add paid SpeedPasses and Special Passes to their individual tickets, tracks each pass's price and consumption status, and allows refunds of unused passes before the ticket's date.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Buy SpeedPasses
`SPEEDPASS CREATE;<ticketId>;<quantity>` SHALL accept quantities 1 and 10 only and create that many independent SpeedPasses for the individual ticket, each with its own identifier, a price equal to 20% of the ticket's stored price (two decimals) and status unused. It SHALL print `OK SPEEDPASS CREATED -> <quantity> passes, <amount charged>`.

#### Scenario: Pack of ten
- **WHEN** ticket `T001` cost 40.00 and `SPEEDPASS CREATE;T001;10` is processed
- **THEN** ten unused SpeedPasses of 8.00 each exist for `T001` and `OK SPEEDPASS CREATED -> 10 passes, 80.00` is printed

#### Scenario: Invalid quantity
- **WHEN** `SPEEDPASS CREATE;T001;5` is processed
- **THEN** no pass is created and an `ERROR` line is printed

### Requirement: Buy Special Pass
`SPECIALPASS CREATE;<ticketId>` SHALL create one Special Pass for the individual ticket priced at the ticket's stored price and print `OK SPECIALPASS CREATED -> <amount charged>`. A ticket that already has a Special Pass, consumed or not, SHALL be rejected with an `ERROR` line.

#### Scenario: Second Special Pass
- **WHEN** `T001` already has a Special Pass and `SPECIALPASS CREATE;T001` is processed
- **THEN** no pass is created and an `ERROR` line is printed

### Requirement: Eligible tickets
Passes SHALL only be bought for existing individual tickets (standalone or generated by a group ticket) dated on or after the system date; group ticket identifiers, unknown identifiers and past tickets SHALL be rejected with an `ERROR` line.

#### Scenario: Ticket inside a group ticket
- **WHEN** `SPEEDPASS CREATE;TG01-1;1` is processed for a current ticket generated by `TG01`
- **THEN** one SpeedPass is created for `TG01-1`

#### Scenario: Group ticket identifier
- **WHEN** `SPEEDPASS CREATE;TG01;1` is processed
- **THEN** an `ERROR` line is printed

### Requirement: Refund unused passes
`SPEEDPASS DELETE;<passId>` and `SPECIALPASS DELETE;<ticketId>` SHALL delete an unused pass and print `OK <SPEEDPASS|SPECIALPASS> DELETED -> refunded <amount>` only if the ticket's date is strictly after the system date. Consumed passes and passes of tickets dated on or before the system date SHALL NOT be refunded.

#### Scenario: Refund before the day
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01, `T001` is dated 2026-10-15 and `SPEEDPASS DELETE;T001-SP1` is processed for an unused pass
- **THEN** the pass is deleted and its price is reported as refunded

#### Scenario: No refund on the day
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-15 and `SPECIALPASS DELETE;T001` is processed
- **THEN** the pass is kept and an `ERROR` line is printed

### Requirement: Consumed passes are kept
A pass SHALL keep its consumption status and remain in the system after being consumed.

#### Scenario: Consumed pass remains
- **WHEN** a pass is marked consumed
- **THEN** it still exists with status consumed and cannot be refunded

### Requirement: Pass persistence
Passes SHALL be stored after every change and restored at startup with their identifier, ticket, type, price and consumption status.

#### Scenario: Restart restores passes
- **WHEN** `T001` has SpeedPasses and a Special Pass and the application is restarted
- **THEN** the same passes exist with the same prices and statuses

### Requirement: Cascade deletion of passes
When a ticket is validly deleted, its passes SHALL be deleted with it: `TICKET DELETE` SHALL delete the ticket's SpeedPasses and Special Pass, and `GROUPTICKET DELETE` SHALL delete the passes of each composing ticket, then those tickets, then the group ticket. All deletion conditions SHALL be checked before anything is removed, so a rejected deletion SHALL leave every ticket and pass unchanged.

#### Scenario: Individual ticket with passes
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01, `T001` (dated 2026-10-15) has SpeedPasses and a Special Pass, and `TICKET DELETE;T001` is processed
- **THEN** `T001` and all its passes no longer exist, also after a restart

#### Scenario: Group ticket with passes
- **WHEN** `TG01` is dated after the system date, `TG01-1` has a SpeedPass and `GROUPTICKET DELETE;TG01` is processed
- **THEN** the SpeedPass, `TG01-1`, `TG01-2` and `TG01` no longer exist

#### Scenario: Rejected deletion
- **WHEN** `TICKET DELETE;T001` is processed on the ticket's date
- **THEN** it is rejected and `T001` keeps all its passes
