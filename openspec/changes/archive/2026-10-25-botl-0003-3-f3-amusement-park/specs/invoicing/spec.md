# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Generates on-demand invoices for users and groups from the tickets and passes recorded in the system, assembling them step by step with a builder.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Invoice commands
`INVOICE CREATE;USER;<correo>` SHALL generate an invoice for an existing user and `INVOICE CREATE;GROUP;<idGrupo>` for an existing group. Unknown recipients or recipient types SHALL be rejected. Invoices SHALL NOT be persisted.

#### Scenario: Unknown user
- **WHEN** `INVOICE CREATE;USER;nadie@correo.com` is executed
- **THEN** an error is reported

#### Scenario: Unknown type
- **WHEN** `INVOICE CREATE;PARK;X` is executed
- **THEN** an error is reported

### Requirement: Builder construction
Invoices SHALL be assembled through a builder abstraction in distinct steps (recipient, sections, items, total), including only the sections applicable to the recipient type and for which data exists; invoice variants SHALL NOT be handled with constructors taking numerous optional parameters.

#### Scenario: User without passes
- **WHEN** a user with one individual ticket and no passes requests an invoice
- **THEN** the invoice contains an individual-tickets section and no passes section

### Requirement: User invoice content
A user invoice SHALL include individual tickets bought directly for that user and the personal passes (SpeedPasses and Special Passes, consumed or not) attached to any of the user's individual tickets, including tickets generated by group tickets. Individual tickets belonging to a group ticket SHALL NOT be listed as individual purchases.

#### Scenario: Passes on group internal ticket
- **WHEN** Ana has ticket T001 (40.00), is a member of group ticket TG01, and bought one SpeedPass (8.00) on her internal ticket of TG01
- **THEN** her invoice lists T001 at 40.00 and the SpeedPass at 8.00, does not list her TG01 internal ticket, and shows total 48.00

### Requirement: Group invoice content
A group invoice SHALL include the group's group-ticket purchases with their total amount, without listing the internal individual tickets or the members' personal passes.

#### Scenario: Group invoice
- **WHEN** group G01 bought TG01 for 108.00 and a member bought passes on an internal ticket
- **THEN** the group invoice lists TG01 at 108.00 and the total is 108.00

### Requirement: Prices and total
Each invoice item SHALL show its price and the invoice SHALL show the total of all included items with two decimals.

#### Scenario: Total shown
- **WHEN** an invoice with items 40.00 and 8.00 is generated
- **THEN** it shows `TOTAL: 48.00`
