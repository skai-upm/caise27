# Proposal

## Why

Visitors want priority access. Delivery 3 adds personal passes sold on top of individual tickets — SpeedPasses (skip the queue, bought one by one or in packs of ten) and a Special Pass (access to special attractions) — each with its own price, persisted state, consumption status and refund rules.

## What Changes

- New pass hierarchy: abstract `PersonalPass` (identifier, price, consumed status) with `SpeedPass` and `SpecialPass`, composed inside the `IndividualTicket` they belong to (standalone tickets and the individual tickets generated by group tickets).
- `SPEEDPASS CREATE;<ticketId>;<1|10>`: one or ten independent SpeedPasses, each priced at 20% of the price stored in the ticket.
- `SPECIALPASS CREATE;<ticketId>`: one Special Pass priced at the ticket's price; at most one per ticket, also once consumed.
- `SPEEDPASS DELETE;<passId>` and `SPECIALPASS DELETE;<ticketId>`: refund unused passes when the ticket date is later than the system date (same rule as ticket refunds); consumed passes stay in the system with their status.
- Purchases and refunds report the amount charged/refunded.
- Passes persist in `data/passes.txt` and are restored onto their tickets.
- User invoices gain a `Personal passes` section (including passes on tickets generated by group tickets).
- The command processor receives the controllers through one `Controllers` holder (keeps its constructor small as controllers grow); `HELP`/`HELP EXTENDED` list the new commands; UML updated.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `personal-passes`: SpeedPasses and Special Passes: pricing, purchase rules, consumption status, refunds and persistence.

### Modified Capabilities
- `invoicing`: user invoices include the user's personal passes.
- `command-processing`: HELP scenario lists the pass commands.

## Impact

- New model classes, `PassManager` controller, `PassRepository`/`TextFilePassRepository`, `Controllers` holder; changes to `IndividualTicket`, `TicketManager`, `InvoiceManager`, `CommandProcessor`, `CommandRegistry`, `Bootstrap`, tests and UML.
