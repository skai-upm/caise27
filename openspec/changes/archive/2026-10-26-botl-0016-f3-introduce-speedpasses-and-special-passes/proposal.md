# Proposal

## Why

Delivery 3 adds paid extras tied to individual tickets: SpeedPasses (skip-the-line uses, bought one at a time or in packs of ten) and a Special Pass for special attractions. They need prices, a consumption status, refunds under the existing date rules, persistence, and must appear on the user's invoice.

## What Changes

- `SPEEDPASS CREATE;<ticketId>;<1|10>` buys 1 or 10 independent SpeedPasses for an individual ticket; each costs 20% of the price stored in that ticket and has its own identifier, price and consumption status.
- `SPECIALPASS CREATE;<ticketId>` buys the ticket's Special Pass at the ticket's price; a ticket can hold at most one (consumed or not).
- Purchases print the amount charged.
- `SPEEDPASS DELETE;<passId>` and `SPECIALPASS DELETE;<ticketId>` refund an unused pass when the ticket date is strictly after the system date (same rule as ticket cancellation).
- Passes are never removed when consumed; they stay with status `consumed` (consumption itself arrives with attraction access in C08).
- Passes apply to any individual ticket, including those generated by a group ticket, as long as the ticket is not in the past.
- Passes persist between executions (`passes.txt`, also under `--fly`).
- The user invoice gains a "Personal passes" section with the passes of all the user's individual tickets, including tickets inside group tickets; group invoices are unchanged.
- Until cascade deletion is introduced (C09), a ticket or group ticket that has passes cannot be deleted; its passes must be refunded first.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `passes`: buying, pricing, refunding and persisting SpeedPasses and Special Passes.

### Modified Capabilities
- `invoices`: user invoices list the user's personal passes.

## Impact

- New model classes `Pass`, `SpeedPass`, `SpecialPass`; `PassController` (singleton), `PassRepository`; `CommandProcessor` (new commands, controllers grouped in a `BusinessControllers` record), `InvoiceController`, `TicketController` deletion guards, `CommandCatalog`, `App`, `uml/model.puml`.
