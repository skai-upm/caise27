# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: User invoice content
A user invoice SHALL include, in an `Individual tickets` section, every individual ticket bought directly for that user with its price, and, in a `Personal passes` section, every personal pass attached to any individual ticket held by the user — including tickets generated by a group ticket — with its price; the total is the sum of both sections. Individual tickets generated by a group ticket SHALL NOT be counted on the user's invoice.

#### Scenario: Direct and group tickets
- **WHEN** Ana bought T001 at 40.00 and T002 at 60.00 and is also a member of group ticket TG01
- **THEN** her invoice lists T001 (40.00) and T002 (60.00), does not list TG01 or its internal tickets, and shows `TOTAL: 100.00`

#### Scenario: Passes on direct and group-generated tickets
- **WHEN** Ana has T001 (40.00) with one SpeedPass (8.00) and her ticket TG01-1 inside group ticket TG01 has a Special Pass (40.00)
- **THEN** her invoice lists T001 under `Individual tickets`, both passes under `Personal passes`, and shows `TOTAL: 88.00`; the group's invoice does not include the passes
