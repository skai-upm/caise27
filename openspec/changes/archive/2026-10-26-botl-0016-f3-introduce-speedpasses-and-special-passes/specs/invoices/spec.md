# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: User invoice
`INVOICE CREATE;USER;<email>` SHALL print an invoice for the registered user with that email listing, with identifier, date and price, the individual tickets bought directly for that user, then the user's personal passes (SpeedPasses and Special Passes of any of the user's individual tickets, including tickets generated by a group ticket) with identifier, type, ticket and price, followed by the total. Individual tickets generated by a group ticket SHALL NOT be listed. An unknown email SHALL produce an `ERROR` line.

#### Scenario: Direct tickets only
- **WHEN** Ana bought `T001` (40.00) and belongs to a group that bought `TG01`
- **THEN** her invoice lists `T001` with 40.00, does not list the tickets of `TG01`, and the total is 40.00

#### Scenario: Passes on a group-ticket ticket
- **WHEN** Ana bought a SpeedPass (8.00) for `TG01-1`, her ticket inside `TG01`
- **THEN** her invoice lists that SpeedPass with 8.00 in the personal passes section but still not `TG01-1` itself
