# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Internal tickets are not deleted individually
`TICKET DELETE` SHALL reject an individual ticket that belongs to a group ticket; such tickets are removed only by deleting their group ticket.

#### Scenario: Delete internal ticket
- **WHEN** `TICKET DELETE` is processed with the identifier of a ticket generated by `TG01`
- **THEN** an error is reported and the ticket remains
