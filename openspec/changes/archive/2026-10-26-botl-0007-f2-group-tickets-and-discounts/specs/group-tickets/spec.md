# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Lets a group buy admission jointly for a date, generating one individual ticket per member, applying volume discounts, and locking the group while the purchase is current or future.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Buy group ticket
`GROUPTICKET CREATE;<id>;<groupId>;<yyyy-MM-dd>` SHALL create a group ticket for an existing group with at least one member and print `OK GROUP TICKET CREATED -> <total>` with two decimals. It SHALL generate one individual ticket for each member at the moment of purchase, each at the current individual ticket price and referring to that member. It SHALL fail with an `ERROR` line if the identifier is empty or used by any ticket, the group does not exist or has no members, the date is invalid, or the date is before the system date.

#### Scenario: Two members
- **WHEN** group `G01` has 2 members, the price is 40.00 and `GROUPTICKET CREATE;TG01;G01;2026-10-15` is processed
- **THEN** two individual tickets for 2026-10-15 are generated, one per member, and `OK GROUP TICKET CREATED -> 80.00` is printed

#### Scenario: Composition fixed at purchase
- **WHEN** a member joins `G01` after `TG01` was bought and its date has passed
- **THEN** `TG01` still contains only the tickets generated at purchase

#### Scenario: Empty or unknown group
- **WHEN** `GROUPTICKET CREATE` is processed for a group without members or a nonexistent group
- **THEN** no ticket is created and an `ERROR` line is printed

### Requirement: Group discount
The group ticket total SHALL be the sum of the generated individual ticket prices reduced by 0% for fewer than 3 members, 10% for 3 to 5, 20% for 6 to 10 and 50% for more than 10, rounded to two decimals.

#### Scenario: Bracket boundaries
- **WHEN** group tickets are bought at an individual price of 40.00 for groups of 2, 3, 5, 6, 10 and 11 members
- **THEN** the totals are 80.00, 108.00, 180.00, 192.00, 320.00 and 220.00

### Requirement: Group lock
While a group has any group ticket dated on or after the system date, `GROUP ADD`, `GROUP REMOVE` and `GROUP DELETE` on that group SHALL be rejected with an `ERROR` line. When all its group tickets are in the past, the group SHALL be modifiable again.

#### Scenario: Locked group
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01, `G01` has group ticket `TG01` for 2026-10-15 and `GROUP ADD;G01;luis@correo.com` is processed
- **THEN** the member is not added and an `ERROR` line is printed

#### Scenario: Lock lifts with time
- **WHEN** the system date is set to 2026-10-16 and `GROUP REMOVE;G01;ana@correo.com` is processed
- **THEN** Ana is removed from `G01`

### Requirement: Members blocked by internal tickets
Individual tickets generated by a group ticket SHALL count as tickets of their users, so a user with such a ticket dated on or after the system date cannot be deleted.

#### Scenario: Member deletion blocked
- **WHEN** Ana's group ticket is dated 2026-10-15, the system date is 2026-10-01 and `USER DELETE;ana@correo.com;Clave1!` is processed
- **THEN** Ana is not deleted and an `ERROR` line is printed

### Requirement: Delete group ticket
`GROUPTICKET DELETE;<id>` SHALL delete the group ticket and all individual tickets generated by it and print `OK GROUP TICKET DELETED`, only if its date is strictly after the system date; otherwise, or if no group ticket has that identifier, it SHALL print an `ERROR` line and delete nothing.

#### Scenario: Future group ticket
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01 and `GROUPTICKET DELETE;TG01` is processed for a group ticket dated 2026-10-15
- **THEN** `TG01` and its individual tickets no longer exist and the group is unlocked

#### Scenario: Group ticket for today
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-15 and `GROUPTICKET DELETE;TG01` is processed
- **THEN** nothing is deleted and an `ERROR` line is printed

### Requirement: Internal tickets are not deleted individually
`TICKET DELETE` SHALL reject an individual ticket that belongs to a group ticket with an `ERROR` line.

#### Scenario: Delete internal ticket
- **WHEN** `TICKET DELETE` is processed with the identifier of a ticket generated by `TG01`
- **THEN** the ticket is kept and an `ERROR` line is printed

### Requirement: Group ticket persistence
Group tickets, their group, date, total and generated individual tickets SHALL be stored after every change and restored at startup.

#### Scenario: Restart restores group ticket
- **WHEN** `TG01` exists and the application is restarted
- **THEN** `TG01` exists with the same total and individual tickets and `G01` is still locked
