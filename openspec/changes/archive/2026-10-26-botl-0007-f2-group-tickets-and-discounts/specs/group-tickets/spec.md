# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Lets a group buy admission for all its members at once with a size-based discount, generating the members' individual tickets and governing the group while the purchase is valid.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Buy a group ticket
The system SHALL create a group ticket with `GROUPTICKET CREATE;Id;IdGrupo;yyyy-MM-dd` for an existing group with at least one member. The identifier SHALL be unique among all tickets and the date SHALL be valid and not earlier than the system date. The response SHALL be `OK GROUP TICKET CREATED -> <Precio>` with the total price formatted with two decimals.

#### Scenario: Purchase for a group
- **WHEN** group `G01` has 3 members, the individual price is 40.00 and `GROUPTICKET CREATE;TG01;G01;2026-10-15` is processed with system date 2026-10-01
- **THEN** the response is `OK GROUP TICKET CREATED -> 108.00`

#### Scenario: Unknown or empty group
- **WHEN** the group does not exist or has no members
- **THEN** an error is reported and no ticket is created

### Requirement: One individual ticket per member
Buying a group ticket SHALL generate one individual ticket for each user that belongs to the group at the time of purchase, dated like the group ticket and priced at the individual price in force. The group ticket SHALL be composed of exactly those tickets; later membership changes SHALL NOT alter it.

#### Scenario: Composition fixed at purchase
- **WHEN** `TG01` is bought for a group with Ana and Pedro, and later (after the ticket date) Luis joins the group
- **THEN** `TG01` still contains exactly the tickets of Ana and Pedro

### Requirement: Group discount
The group ticket total SHALL be the sum of its individual ticket prices minus a discount by number of members: fewer than 3 → 0%, 3 to 5 → 10%, 6 to 10 → 20%, more than 10 → 50%.

#### Scenario: Discount brackets and boundaries
- **WHEN** the individual price is 40.00 and groups of 2, 3, 5, 6, 10 and 11 members buy group tickets
- **THEN** the totals are 80.00, 108.00, 180.00, 192.00, 320.00 and 220.00 respectively

### Requirement: Delete a future group ticket
The system SHALL delete a group ticket with `GROUPTICKET DELETE;Id` only when its date is strictly later than the system date, removing also all its internal individual tickets; otherwise an error SHALL be reported and nothing deleted.

#### Scenario: Future group ticket
- **WHEN** the system date is before the ticket date and `GROUPTICKET DELETE;TG01` is processed
- **THEN** `TG01` and its individual tickets no longer exist

#### Scenario: Current or past group ticket
- **WHEN** the system date is on or after the ticket date
- **THEN** the deletion is rejected and all tickets remain

### Requirement: Internal tickets protect their users
The individual tickets generated by a group ticket SHALL prevent their users from being deleted while dated on or after the system date, exactly like ordinary individual tickets.

#### Scenario: Member with group ticket
- **WHEN** Ana's group bought `TG01` for 2026-10-15, the system date is 2026-10-15 and `USER DELETE` is processed for Ana
- **THEN** the deletion is rejected

### Requirement: Common ticket abstraction
Individual and group tickets SHALL be manageable through a common ticket abstraction exposing identifier, date, price and the users it admits; identifiers SHALL be unique across both kinds.

#### Scenario: Shared identifier space
- **WHEN** an individual ticket `T001` exists and `GROUPTICKET CREATE;T001;G01;2026-10-15` is processed
- **THEN** an error is reported

### Requirement: Group ticket persistence
Group tickets SHALL be stored and restored with their identifier, group, date, total price and internal individual tickets.

#### Scenario: Restart
- **WHEN** `TG01` is bought and the application restarts
- **THEN** `TG01` exists with the same group, date, total price and individual tickets

### Requirement: Group ticket commands in HELP
`HELP` SHALL list `GROUPTICKET CREATE;Id;IdGrupo;yyyy-MM-dd` and `GROUPTICKET DELETE;Id`.

#### Scenario: Help shows group ticket commands
- **WHEN** `HELP` is processed
- **THEN** the output contains both syntaxes
