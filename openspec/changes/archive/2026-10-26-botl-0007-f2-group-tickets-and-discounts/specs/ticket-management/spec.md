# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Delete individual ticket
The command `TICKET DELETE;<id>` SHALL delete the ticket and print `OK TICKET DELETED` only when the ticket date is strictly later than the system date; a ticket dated on or before the system date, an unknown identifier, or an individual ticket that belongs to a group ticket SHALL be rejected with an `ERROR` line.

#### Scenario: Future ticket deleted
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01 and ticket `T001` is dated 2026-10-15
- **THEN** `TICKET DELETE;T001` deletes it and prints `OK TICKET DELETED`

#### Scenario: Ticket for today
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-15 and ticket `T001` is dated 2026-10-15
- **THEN** `TICKET DELETE;T001` is rejected with an `ERROR` line

#### Scenario: Past ticket
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-16 and ticket `T001` is dated 2026-10-15
- **THEN** `TICKET DELETE;T001` is rejected with an `ERROR` line and the ticket is kept

#### Scenario: Internal ticket of a group ticket
- **WHEN** `TICKET DELETE` names an individual ticket generated by a group ticket
- **THEN** it is rejected with an `ERROR` line and the group ticket is unchanged

### Requirement: Ticket persistence
Individual tickets and group tickets SHALL be stored in text files and restored when the application starts, linked to the loaded user and group objects. Individual tickets keep identifier, date, user, price charged and, for internal tickets, their group ticket; group tickets keep identifier, group, date, total price and their composition. Past tickets SHALL be retained as historical records and SHALL NOT be removed when the system date moves past them.

#### Scenario: Tickets survive restart
- **WHEN** ticket `T001` is created, the application terminates and starts again
- **THEN** `T001` exists with the same date, user and price

#### Scenario: Group tickets survive restart
- **WHEN** group ticket `TG01` for a group of three is created and the application restarts
- **THEN** `TG01` exists with the same group, date and total, composed of the same three individual tickets referring to the same users

#### Scenario: Past tickets retained
- **WHEN** the system date is moved after the date of ticket `T001`
- **THEN** `T001` still exists

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Common ticket abstraction
Individual and group tickets SHALL be handled through a common ticket abstraction exposing identifier, date, price and the users the ticket covers (one user for an individual ticket, the users of its internal tickets for a group ticket). Ticket identifiers SHALL be unique across both kinds.

#### Scenario: Shared identifier space
- **WHEN** individual ticket `T001` exists and `GROUPTICKET CREATE;T001;G01;2026-10-15` is processed
- **THEN** no group ticket is created and an `ERROR` line is printed

### Requirement: Create group ticket
The command `GROUPTICKET CREATE;<id>;<groupId>;<yyyy-MM-dd>` SHALL create a group ticket for an existing group with at least one member, generating one individual ticket for each member at that moment, each with the group ticket's date, referring to its user and storing the current individual price. It SHALL print `OK GROUP TICKET CREATED -> <total>` with two decimals. It SHALL fail with an `ERROR` line, creating nothing, if the group does not exist or is empty, the identifier is blank or already used, or the date is invalid or earlier than the system date.

#### Scenario: One ticket per member
- **WHEN** group `G01` has Ana, Pedro and Luis and `GROUPTICKET CREATE;TG01;G01;2026-10-15` succeeds
- **THEN** `TG01` is composed of three individual tickets dated 2026-10-15, one referring to each member

#### Scenario: Composition is a snapshot
- **WHEN** `TG01` was bought for Ana and Pedro and, after its date has passed, Luis is added to `G01`
- **THEN** `TG01` still covers only Ana and Pedro

#### Scenario: Empty or unknown group
- **WHEN** `GROUPTICKET CREATE` names a group with no members or a group that does not exist
- **THEN** no ticket is created and an `ERROR` line is printed

### Requirement: Group discount
The total price of a group ticket SHALL be the current individual price multiplied by the number of members, reduced by a discount that depends on the number of members: fewer than 3 members 0%, 3 to 5 members 10%, 6 to 10 members 20%, more than 10 members 50%. The total is rounded to two decimals.

#### Scenario: Discount brackets at 40.00
- **WHEN** the individual price is 40.00 and group tickets are bought for 2, 3, 5, 6, 10 and 11 members
- **THEN** the totals are 80.00, 108.00, 180.00, 192.00, 320.00 and 220.00 respectively

### Requirement: Delete group ticket
The command `GROUPTICKET DELETE;<id>` SHALL delete the group ticket and all its internal individual tickets, printing `OK GROUP TICKET DELETED`, only when its date is strictly later than the system date; otherwise, or for an unknown identifier, it SHALL print an `ERROR` line and delete nothing.

#### Scenario: Future group ticket deleted
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01 and `TG01` is dated 2026-10-15
- **THEN** `GROUPTICKET DELETE;TG01` removes `TG01` and its internal tickets

#### Scenario: Current group ticket kept
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-15 and `TG01` is dated 2026-10-15
- **THEN** `GROUPTICKET DELETE;TG01` is rejected and nothing is deleted

### Requirement: Internal tickets protect users
The individual tickets generated by a group ticket SHALL count as tickets of their users: while dated on or after the system date they prevent deleting those users. When a user with only past tickets is deleted, their past internal tickets SHALL be removed from the group tickets that contain them.

#### Scenario: Member with current group ticket
- **WHEN** Ana's internal ticket in `TG01` is dated 2026-10-15 and the system date is 2026-10-10
- **THEN** `USER DELETE` for Ana is rejected with an `ERROR` line

#### Scenario: Member with past group ticket
- **WHEN** the system date is after `TG01`'s date and Ana is deleted
- **THEN** `TG01` no longer contains Ana's internal ticket
