# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Ticket data
Every individual ticket SHALL have a unique identifier, a date of use, a holder that is a reference to the registered user who owns it, and the price charged when it was bought. Individual and group tickets SHALL be handled through a common ticket abstraction (identifier, date, price, the users it admits) and share one identifier namespace, so an identifier used by either kind cannot be reused by the other.

#### Scenario: Ticket holds user and price
- **WHEN** `TICKET CREATE;T001;ana@correo.com;2026-10-15` succeeds while the price is 40.00
- **THEN** ticket T001 has date 2026-10-15, holder Ana and price 40.00

#### Scenario: Shared identifier namespace
- **WHEN** group ticket TG01 exists and `TICKET CREATE;TG01;ana@correo.com;2026-10-15` is processed
- **THEN** the response is `ERROR DUPLICATE TICKET`

### Requirement: Refund individual ticket
`TICKET DELETE;<id>` SHALL delete the ticket and respond `OK TICKET DELETED` only when its date is strictly later than the system date; a ticket dated today or earlier SHALL be kept and the response is `ERROR TICKET NOT REFUNDABLE`. An unknown identifier is rejected with `ERROR TICKET NOT FOUND`. Individual tickets generated by a group ticket, and group tickets themselves, cannot be deleted with `TICKET DELETE` (`ERROR TICKET NOT FOUND`); they are refunded only through `GROUPTICKET DELETE`.

#### Scenario: Future ticket deleted
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01 and `TICKET DELETE;T001` is processed for a ticket dated 2026-10-15
- **THEN** the response is `OK TICKET DELETED`

#### Scenario: Ticket for today
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-15 and `TICKET DELETE;T001` is processed for a ticket dated 2026-10-15
- **THEN** the response is `ERROR TICKET NOT REFUNDABLE` and the ticket remains

#### Scenario: Internal ticket of a group ticket
- **WHEN** `TICKET DELETE;TG01-1` is processed for an individual ticket generated by group ticket TG01
- **THEN** the response is `ERROR TICKET NOT FOUND` and the group ticket is unchanged
