# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Lets a group buy tickets jointly: a group ticket is composed of one individual ticket per member and is priced with a size-based discount.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Buy group ticket
The system SHALL accept `GROUPTICKET CREATE;<id>;<grupo>;yyyy-MM-dd` and create a group ticket linked to the group, generating one individual ticket per member at the moment of purchase, each referencing its user and priced at the current individual price. Unknown groups, empty groups, duplicate identifiers, invalid dates and dates before the system date SHALL be rejected. On success it SHALL report `OK GROUP TICKET CREATED -> <precio>` with two decimals.

#### Scenario: Buy group ticket
- **WHEN** G01 has 3 members, the price is 40.00 and `GROUPTICKET CREATE;TG01;G01;2026-10-15` is processed
- **THEN** the system reports `OK GROUP TICKET CREATED -> 108.00` and TG01 contains 3 individual tickets for 2026-10-15, one per member

#### Scenario: Composition is fixed at purchase
- **WHEN** TG01 was bought for G01 with 3 members and later, after TG01's date, a member is added to G01
- **THEN** TG01 still contains exactly the 3 original tickets

#### Scenario: Empty group
- **WHEN** G02 has no members and `GROUPTICKET CREATE;TG02;G02;2026-10-15` is processed
- **THEN** the system reports an error

### Requirement: Group discount
The group ticket price SHALL be the sum of its individual ticket prices minus a discount by member count: fewer than 3 → 0%, 3 to 5 → 10%, 6 to 10 → 20%, more than 10 → 50%.

#### Scenario: Discount brackets and boundaries
- **WHEN** group tickets are bought with an individual price of 40.00 for groups of 2, 3, 5, 6, 10 and 11 members
- **THEN** their prices are 80.00, 108.00, 180.00, 192.00, 320.00 and 220.00

### Requirement: Generated tickets block user deletion
The individual tickets generated by a group ticket SHALL count as tickets of their users: while dated on or after the system date they SHALL prevent those users from being deleted.

#### Scenario: Member cannot be deleted
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01, Ana is in TG01 for 2026-10-15 and `USER DELETE;ana@correo.com;Clave1!` is processed
- **THEN** the system reports an error and Ana still exists

### Requirement: Delete group ticket
The system SHALL accept `GROUPTICKET DELETE;<id>` and, only when its date is strictly later than the system date, delete the group ticket together with all the individual tickets it generated.

#### Scenario: Delete future group ticket
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01 and `GROUPTICKET DELETE;TG01` is processed for TG01 dated 2026-10-15
- **THEN** the system reports `OK GROUP TICKET DELETED`, TG01 and its individual tickets no longer exist, and G01 is no longer locked

#### Scenario: Group ticket for today
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-15 and `GROUPTICKET DELETE;TG01` is processed
- **THEN** the system reports an error and nothing is deleted

### Requirement: Group ticket persistence
Group tickets SHALL be stored with identifier, group, date, total price and their generated individual tickets, and restored at startup with the same composition.

#### Scenario: Group tickets survive restart
- **WHEN** TG01 exists and the application restarts
- **THEN** TG01 exists with the same group, date, price and individual tickets, and G01 is still locked while TG01 is current or future
