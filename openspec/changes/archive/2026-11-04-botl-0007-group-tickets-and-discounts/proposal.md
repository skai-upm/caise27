# Proposal

## Why

Groups exist but can only buy tickets member by member. A group ticket must create one individual ticket per member at purchase time, apply a size-based discount, and lock the group and its members while it is current or future. This introduces ticket specialisation and composition.

## What Changes

- Common ticket abstraction: an abstract `Ticket` (identifier, date, price) specialised by `IndividualTicket` (the existing individual ticket, renamed) and the new `GroupTicket`.
- `GROUPTICKET CREATE;TG01;G01;2026-10-15` buys a group ticket for a non-empty group on a date on or after the system date. It generates one individual ticket per member of the group **at that moment**, each at the current individual price. It replies `OK GROUP TICKET CREATED -> <Precio>`, where the price is the sum of the individual prices minus the discount: fewer than 3 members 0%, 3–5 members 10%, 6–10 members 20%, more than 10 members 50%.
- `GROUPTICKET DELETE;TG001` deletes a group ticket only if its date is later than the system date, together with all its individual tickets.
- While a group has a group ticket dated on or after the system date, members cannot be added or removed and the group cannot be deleted. When all its group tickets are in the past, the group can be modified again.
- The generated individual tickets block deletion of their users while current or future, like ordinary tickets.
- Group tickets persist in their own text file and are restored at startup, linked to the existing group and users.
- `HELP` and the PlantUML model (inheritance, composition, aggregation, multiplicities) are updated.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `group-tickets`: group ticket purchase, discount brackets, composition snapshot, deletion, group lock, shared ticket identifiers.
- `group-ticket-file-persistence`: group ticket file format and restart recovery.

### Modified Capabilities
- `user-management`: "Delete user" also counts the individual tickets generated by group tickets.
- `application-control`: `HELP` lists `GROUPTICKET CREATE` and `GROUPTICKET DELETE`.
- `domain-model-diagram`: the diagram scenarios name the renamed and new ticket classes and their inheritance/composition.

## Impact

- `Ticket` becomes abstract. The existing class is renamed `IndividualTicket` (no behaviour change). New `GroupTicket` and `GroupTicketFileRepository`. `TicketManager` manages both kinds. `CommandProcessor` checks the group lock before `GROUP ADD/REMOVE/DELETE`.
- `tickets.txt` format unchanged; new `grouptickets.txt` (git-ignored). No new dependencies.
- Out of scope: attraction admission.
