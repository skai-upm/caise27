# Proposal

## Why

Visitors want personal add-ons on top of their tickets: SpeedPasses, sold singly or in packs of ten, and one Special Pass per ticket. Each pass needs its own identity, price, consumption status, persistence and refund rule, priced from the ticket it belongs to.

## What Changes

- New pass hierarchy: abstract `Pass` (identifier, price, ticket, consumed) with `SpeedPass` and `SpecialPass`. Passes belong to individual tickets, both direct and generated by group tickets.
- `SPEEDPASS CREATE;IdCompra;IdTicket;1|10` creates 1 or 10 independent SpeedPasses `IdCompra-1…n` at 20% of the ticket's stored price each, printing `OK SPEEDPASS CREATED -> <total>`. All-or-nothing on identifier conflicts.
- `SPECIALPASS CREATE;IdPase;IdTicket` creates at most one Special Pass per ticket, ever recorded, consumed or not, at 100% of the ticket's stored price. It prints `OK SPECIALPASS CREATED -> <precio>`.
- `SPEEDPASS DELETE;IdPase` / `SPECIALPASS DELETE;IdPase` refund an unconsumed pass whose ticket is dated later than the system date. Consumed passes stay recorded and are not refundable.
- Passes are persisted in `passes.txt` and restored at startup.
- The tickets generated by a group ticket now store the discounted per-member price (36.00 for 3 members at 40.00), so passes on them follow the discounted price. Group totals and outputs are unchanged.
- User invoices gain a personal-passes section, including passes on group-ticket members' tickets. Group invoices never include member passes. This completes the rule deferred in the invoice change.
- Until cascade deletion is introduced, a ticket or group ticket whose individual tickets have passes cannot be deleted.
- `HELP`/`HELP EXTENDED` and the UML are updated.
- Pass consumption at attractions is not part of this change; it belongs to the pass-based access change.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `passes`: pass data, purchase, pricing, refund, and protection of tickets that have passes.
- `pass-file-persistence`: pass file format and restart recovery.

### Modified Capabilities
- `group-tickets`: constituent tickets store the discounted per-member price.
- `invoices`: user invoices list personal passes, and group invoices explicitly exclude them.
- `application-control`: `HELP` lists the four pass commands.

## Impact

- New `business.model` classes `Pass`, `SpeedPass`, `SpecialPass`, a new `business.service.PassManager`, and a new `persistence.PassFileRepository`. `TicketManager` gains `findIndividualTicket`. `GroupTicket` computes the member price. `ParkController`, `CommandProcessor`, `CommandCatalog`, the invoice builders/director and `App` wiring are updated.
- New data file `passes.txt` (git-ignored). Existing files are unchanged.
