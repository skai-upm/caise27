# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: User invoice content
A user invoice SHALL list, in an individual-tickets section, each individual ticket bought directly for that user with `TICKET CREATE` (identifier, date and price), and, in a personal-passes section, each SpeedPass and Special Pass belonging to any individual ticket of that user, including tickets generated by a group ticket (identifier, kind, ticket and price). It SHALL show as total the sum of the listed prices. Individual tickets generated by a group ticket SHALL NOT be listed or counted in a user invoice.

#### Scenario: Direct ticket counted once
- **GIVEN** Ana has ticket `T001` (40.00) bought with `TICKET CREATE` and is also a member of a group whose group ticket `TG01` contains a ticket for her
- **WHEN** `INVOICE CREATE;USER;ana@correo.com` is processed
- **THEN** the invoice lists `T001` with 40.00, does not list any ticket of `TG01`, and the total is 40.00

#### Scenario: Prices as charged
- **GIVEN** Ana bought `T001` at 40.00 and, after `SYSTEM PRICE;60.00`, `T002` at 60.00
- **WHEN** her invoice is generated
- **THEN** it lists 40.00 and 60.00 and the total is 100.00

#### Scenario: Personal passes on a group-ticket member
- **GIVEN** Ana's ticket generated by group ticket `TG01` (stored price 36.00) has SpeedPass `SP1-1` (7.20)
- **WHEN** `INVOICE CREATE;USER;ana@correo.com` is processed
- **THEN** the invoice lists `SP1-1` with 7.20 in the personal-passes section, does not list the `TG01` ticket, and `SP1-1` is not added to the invoice of the group

### Requirement: Group invoice content
A group invoice SHALL list, in a group-tickets section, each group ticket bought by that group (identifier, date, number of members and its charged total price), and SHALL show as total the sum of those prices. It SHALL NOT list or add the individual tickets composing the group tickets, nor any purchase made individually by its members, including their personal passes.

#### Scenario: Group ticket amount only
- **GIVEN** group `G01` with three members bought `TG01` for 108.00 and one member also bought `T001` individually
- **WHEN** `INVOICE CREATE;GROUP;G01` is processed
- **THEN** the invoice lists `TG01` with 108.00, does not list `T001` or the constituent tickets, and the total is 108.00

#### Scenario: Member passes excluded
- **GIVEN** a member of `G01` bought a SpeedPass for their `TG01` ticket
- **WHEN** `INVOICE CREATE;GROUP;G01` is processed
- **THEN** the pass is not listed and the total is still 108.00
