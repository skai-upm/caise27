# Proposal

## Why

The park records purchases (individual tickets, group tickets) but cannot tell a user or a group what they have been charged. Invoices must be generated on demand from that information, and their variable structure (different recipients, optional sections) is the occasion to formally apply the Builder pattern instead of constructors with many optional parameters.

## What Changes

- `INVOICE CREATE;USER;<email>` and `INVOICE CREATE;GROUP;<groupId>` generate and print an invoice; invoices are not persisted.
- A Builder abstraction (`InvoiceBuilder` interface + concrete builder) assembles the invoice in distinct steps: recipient, issue date, sections and their lines; the total is computed at build time; sections with no data are left out.
- User invoice: the individual tickets bought directly for that user (tickets generated by a `GROUPTICKET` are not counted again). Personal passes do not exist yet; the pass section will appear once passes are introduced (they may then also be attached to internal tickets).
- Group invoice: the group's `GROUPTICKET` purchases with their discounted amounts, without repeating the composing individual tickets.
- Each line shows its price and the invoice shows a total.
- `HELP` lists the new commands; `uml/model.puml` includes the invoice classes and the builder abstraction.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `invoicing`: Invoice generation for users and groups, its sections and totals, and its construction through a builder.

### Modified Capabilities
- `command-processing`: "Terminal command input" generalises multi-line responses (invoices are multi-line); "HELP command" adds a scenario for the INVOICE commands.

## Impact

- New model classes `Invoice`, `InvoiceSection`, `InvoiceLine`, `InvoiceBuilder`, `StandardInvoiceBuilder`; new controller `InvoiceManager`; `TicketManager` gains queries by user/group; `CommandProcessor`, `App`, `Park` fixture and UML updated.
