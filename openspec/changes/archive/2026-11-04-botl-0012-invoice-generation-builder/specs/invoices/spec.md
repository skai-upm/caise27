# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Generates on-demand invoices for users and groups from the purchases stored in the system, built step by step so that each invoice contains only the sections that apply to its recipient.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Invoice commands
The system SHALL process `INVOICE CREATE;USER;<correo>` for an existing user and `INVOICE CREATE;GROUP;<identificadorGrupo>` for an existing group, printing the invoice as a first line starting with `OK` that identifies the recipient, followed by one line per section title and item and a final total line. The recipient type `USER` or `GROUP` is case-insensitive. A command with an unknown recipient type, a non-existent user or group, or a parameter count other than two SHALL print a line starting with `ERROR` and no invoice.

#### Scenario: Unknown recipient
- **WHEN** `INVOICE CREATE;USER;nadie@correo.com` or `INVOICE CREATE;GROUP;G99` is processed and that user or group does not exist
- **THEN** the result starts with `ERROR` and no invoice lines are printed

#### Scenario: Malformed command
- **WHEN** `INVOICE CREATE;COMPANY;X`, `INVOICE CREATE;USER` or `INVOICE CREATE;USER;a@b.com;extra` is processed
- **THEN** the result starts with `ERROR`

### Requirement: User invoice content
A user invoice SHALL list, in an individual-tickets section, each individual ticket bought directly for that user with `TICKET CREATE` (identifier, date and price), and SHALL show as total the sum of the listed prices. Individual tickets generated by a group ticket SHALL NOT be listed or counted in a user invoice.

#### Scenario: Direct ticket counted once
- **GIVEN** Ana has ticket `T001` (40.00) bought with `TICKET CREATE` and is also a member of a group whose group ticket `TG01` contains a ticket for her
- **WHEN** `INVOICE CREATE;USER;ana@correo.com` is processed
- **THEN** the invoice lists `T001` with 40.00, does not list any ticket of `TG01`, and the total is 40.00

#### Scenario: Prices as charged
- **GIVEN** Ana bought `T001` at 40.00 and, after `SYSTEM PRICE;60.00`, `T002` at 60.00
- **WHEN** her invoice is generated
- **THEN** it lists 40.00 and 60.00 and the total is 100.00

### Requirement: Group invoice content
A group invoice SHALL list, in a group-tickets section, each group ticket bought by that group (identifier, date, number of members and its charged total price), and SHALL show as total the sum of those prices. It SHALL NOT list or add the individual tickets composing the group tickets, nor any purchase made individually by its members.

#### Scenario: Group ticket amount only
- **GIVEN** group `G01` with three members bought `TG01` for 108.00 and one member also bought `T001` individually
- **WHEN** `INVOICE CREATE;GROUP;G01` is processed
- **THEN** the invoice lists `TG01` with 108.00, does not list `T001` or the constituent tickets, and the total is 108.00

### Requirement: Applicable sections only
An invoice SHALL include only the sections for which its recipient has data. A recipient without purchases SHALL get an invoice with its header and a total of 0.00 and no item section.

#### Scenario: No purchases
- **GIVEN** user Pedro has bought nothing directly
- **WHEN** `INVOICE CREATE;USER;pedro@correo.com` is processed
- **THEN** the invoice shows Pedro's header and `TOTAL: 0.00` and contains no individual-tickets section

### Requirement: Builder-based construction
Invoices SHALL be assembled through a common builder abstraction with separate steps for the header, the item sections and the total, with one concrete builder per recipient type driven by a common director. Invoice variants SHALL NOT be created through constructors with numerous optional parameters. Generated invoices SHALL NOT be persisted.

#### Scenario: Same steps, different builders
- **WHEN** the director constructs an invoice with the user builder and with the group builder
- **THEN** both are produced by the same sequence of steps and differ only in the sections each builder contributes

#### Scenario: Nothing stored
- **WHEN** invoices are generated
- **THEN** no data file is created or changed
