# Proposal

## Why

Users and groups buy tickets, but there is no way to see what they have been charged. On-demand invoices must list only the applicable charges without double counting, for example a group ticket and its member tickets. Invoices also have to be built step by step with the Builder pattern instead of constructors with many optional parameters.

## What Changes

- New commands `INVOICE CREATE;USER;<correo>` and `INVOICE CREATE;GROUP;<identificadorGrupo>`.
- A Builder abstraction (`InvoiceBuilder`) with distinct construction steps (header, ticket section, total), two concrete builders (user, group) and a director that runs the steps. The result is an immutable `Invoice` made of sections and priced lines plus a total.
- A user invoice lists the individual tickets bought directly for that user (`TICKET CREATE`) with their prices. Tickets generated by a group ticket are not charged again.
- A group invoice lists the group's group tickets with their total prices. Constituent tickets are not listed separately.
- Sections without content are omitted. Invoices are not persisted.
- Unknown users or groups are reported with `ERROR` and no invoice is produced.
- `HELP` and the PlantUML model are updated.
- Personal passes do not exist yet (they arrive in a later Delivery 3 change). Their invoice rules are recorded in the design for completion then, and nothing about passes is implemented now.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `invoices`: invoice commands, Builder-based construction, content rules for users and groups, totals, omission of empty sections.

### Modified Capabilities
- `application-control`: `HELP` lists the two `INVOICE CREATE` forms.

## Impact

- New package `business.invoice` (`Invoice`, `InvoiceSection`, `InvoiceLine`, `InvoiceBuilder`, `UserInvoiceBuilder`, `GroupInvoiceBuilder`, `InvoiceDirector`).
- `ParkController` gains `createUserInvoice`/`createGroupInvoice`, and `CommandProcessor` parses the command and prints the invoice.
- No data file changes and no new dependencies.
- Delivery milestone message: `[F3-C02] Add invoice generation with builder`.
