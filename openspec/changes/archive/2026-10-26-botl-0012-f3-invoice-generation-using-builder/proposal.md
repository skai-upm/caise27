# Proposal

## Why

The park records what each user and group has bought but cannot produce a bill. Invoices differ by recipient (user or group) and by the data available, which makes a constructor with many optional parameters fragile; Delivery 3 asks to assemble them step by step with the Builder pattern.

## What Changes

- New commands `INVOICE CREATE;USER;<correo>` and `INVOICE CREATE;GROUP;<identificadorGrupo>` that build and print an invoice on demand (never persisted).
- Builder pattern: an abstract `InvoiceBuilder` with one step per invoice part, concrete `UserInvoiceBuilder` and `GroupInvoiceBuilder` implementing only the steps applicable to their recipient, and an `InvoiceDirector` that runs the steps in order. The product `Invoice` (sections of lines, total) is immutable.
- User invoice: header plus the individual tickets bought directly for the user. Internal tickets generated by a group ticket are not counted as the user's purchases.
- Group invoice: header plus the group's group-ticket purchases with their discounted totals; internal tickets are not listed again.
- Sections without data are omitted; every line shows its price; the invoice shows a total. Issue date = system date.
- Personal passes are not part of the system yet; the step-based builder lets a future passes section be added without changing existing steps.
- HELP lists the new commands; UML updated.
- Required commit title: `[F3-C02] Add invoice generation with builder`.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `invoicing`: On-demand invoices for users and groups, their contents and the counting rules.

### Modified Capabilities
- `command-processing`: HELP lists the INVOICE commands.

## Impact

- New model classes `Invoice`, `InvoiceSection`, `InvoiceLine`; new controller classes `InvoiceBuilder`, `UserInvoiceBuilder`, `GroupInvoiceBuilder`, `InvoiceDirector`; `ParkController` gains invoice operations; `CommandProcessor` routes and formats `INVOICE CREATE`.
- No persistence changes.
