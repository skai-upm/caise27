# Proposal

## Why

Users and groups need a statement of what they have bought. Invoices vary by recipient (user or group) and by which purchases exist, which makes them a natural fit for the Builder pattern instead of constructors with many optional parameters.

## What Changes

- New commands `INVOICE CREATE;USER;<email>` and `INVOICE CREATE;GROUP;<groupId>` that print an invoice generated on demand (never persisted).
- A Builder abstraction assembles the invoice step by step: header for the recipient, one section per applicable kind of purchase, lines with prices, and the total.
- User invoice: the individual tickets bought directly for that user. Individual tickets generated by a group ticket are not counted again.
- Group invoice: the group's group-ticket purchases with their (discounted) amounts; the internal individual tickets are not listed again.
- Only sections with data are included; an invoice with no purchases states it and totals 0.00.
- Personal passes do not exist yet; when they are introduced (Delivery 3, C06) the user invoice gains a passes section through a new builder step.
- PlantUML model and HELP updated.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `invoices`: on-demand invoice generation for users and groups.
