# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Generates on-demand invoices for users and groups listing their purchases with prices and a total, without counting any purchase twice.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: User invoice
`INVOICE CREATE;USER;<email>` SHALL print an invoice for the registered user with that email listing, with identifier, date and price, the individual tickets bought directly for that user, followed by the total. Individual tickets generated by a group ticket SHALL NOT be listed. An unknown email SHALL produce an `ERROR` line.

#### Scenario: Direct tickets only
- **WHEN** Ana bought `T001` (40.00) and belongs to a group that bought `TG01`
- **THEN** her invoice lists `T001` with 40.00, does not list the tickets of `TG01`, and the total is 40.00

### Requirement: Group invoice
`INVOICE CREATE;GROUP;<groupId>` SHALL print an invoice for the group listing its group-ticket purchases with identifier, date, number of members, discount and amount, followed by the total. The individual tickets composing them SHALL NOT be listed again. An unknown group SHALL produce an `ERROR` line.

#### Scenario: Group tickets with discount
- **WHEN** group `G01` with 3 members bought `TG01` at 40.00 per member
- **THEN** the invoice lists `TG01` with amount 108.00 and the total is 108.00

### Requirement: Applicable sections only
An invoice SHALL include only the sections that apply to its recipient type and have data; an invoice without purchases SHALL say there are no billable items and show a total of 0.00.

#### Scenario: User with nothing bought
- **WHEN** `INVOICE CREATE;USER;luis@correo.com` is processed for a user without tickets
- **THEN** the invoice has no ticket section, states that there are no billable items and shows `TOTAL: 0.00`

### Requirement: Invoice format and lifetime
Each invoice SHALL start with `OK INVOICE`, show the recipient, show every included item's price and end with `TOTAL: <amount>` with two decimals. Invoices SHALL be generated on demand and SHALL NOT be persisted. An invalid recipient type SHALL produce an `ERROR` line.

#### Scenario: Unknown recipient type
- **WHEN** `INVOICE CREATE;PARK;X` is processed
- **THEN** an `ERROR` line is printed
