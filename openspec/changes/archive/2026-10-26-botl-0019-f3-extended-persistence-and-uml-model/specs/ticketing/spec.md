# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Refund individual ticket
`TICKET DELETE;<id>` SHALL delete the ticket and respond `OK TICKET DELETED` only when its date is strictly later than the system date; a ticket dated today or earlier SHALL be kept and the response is `ERROR TICKET NOT REFUNDABLE`. An unknown identifier is rejected with `ERROR TICKET NOT FOUND`. Individual tickets generated by a group ticket, and group tickets themselves, cannot be deleted with `TICKET DELETE` (`ERROR TICKET NOT FOUND`); they are refunded only through `GROUPTICKET DELETE`. Deleting a ticket SHALL also delete all its SpeedPasses and its Special Pass; if the deletion cannot be completed, neither the ticket nor any of its passes is deleted.

#### Scenario: Future ticket deleted
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01 and `TICKET DELETE;T001` is processed for a ticket dated 2026-10-15
- **THEN** the response is `OK TICKET DELETED`

#### Scenario: Ticket for today
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-15 and `TICKET DELETE;T001` is processed for a ticket dated 2026-10-15
- **THEN** the response is `ERROR TICKET NOT REFUNDABLE` and the ticket remains

#### Scenario: Internal ticket of a group ticket
- **WHEN** `TICKET DELETE;TG01-1` is processed for an individual ticket generated by group ticket TG01
- **THEN** the response is `ERROR TICKET NOT FOUND` and the group ticket is unchanged

#### Scenario: Passes deleted with the ticket
- **WHEN** future ticket T001 has SpeedPasses and a Special Pass and `TICKET DELETE;T001` succeeds
- **THEN** none of its passes exists any more, also after restarting
