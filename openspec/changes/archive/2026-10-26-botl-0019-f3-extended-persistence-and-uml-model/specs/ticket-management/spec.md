# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Delete individual ticket
The command `TICKET DELETE;<id>` SHALL delete the ticket together with all its SpeedPasses and its Special Pass, and print `OK TICKET DELETED`, only when the ticket date is strictly later than the system date; a ticket dated on or before the system date, an unknown identifier, or an individual ticket that belongs to a group ticket SHALL be rejected with an `ERROR` line.

#### Scenario: Future ticket deleted
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01 and ticket `T001` is dated 2026-10-15
- **THEN** `TICKET DELETE;T001` deletes it and prints `OK TICKET DELETED`

#### Scenario: Passes deleted with the ticket
- **WHEN** future ticket `T001` has SpeedPasses `SP1`–`SP10` and Special Pass `XP1` and `TICKET DELETE;T001` succeeds
- **THEN** none of those passes exists any longer, also after a restart

#### Scenario: Ticket for today
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-15 and ticket `T001` is dated 2026-10-15
- **THEN** `TICKET DELETE;T001` is rejected with an `ERROR` line

#### Scenario: Past ticket
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-16 and ticket `T001` is dated 2026-10-15
- **THEN** `TICKET DELETE;T001` is rejected with an `ERROR` line and the ticket is kept

#### Scenario: Internal ticket of a group ticket
- **WHEN** `TICKET DELETE` names an individual ticket generated by a group ticket
- **THEN** it is rejected with an `ERROR` line and the group ticket is unchanged

### Requirement: Delete group ticket
The command `GROUPTICKET DELETE;<id>` SHALL, only when the group ticket's date is strictly later than the system date, delete first the passes of each of its internal individual tickets, then those individual tickets, and finally the group ticket, printing `OK GROUP TICKET DELETED`; otherwise, or for an unknown identifier, it SHALL print an `ERROR` line and delete nothing.

#### Scenario: Future group ticket deleted
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01 and `TG01` is dated 2026-10-15
- **THEN** `GROUPTICKET DELETE;TG01` removes `TG01` and its internal tickets

#### Scenario: Internal passes deleted
- **WHEN** internal tickets of future `TG01` have SpeedPasses and a Special Pass and `TG01` is deleted
- **THEN** those passes, the internal tickets and `TG01` no longer exist, also after a restart

#### Scenario: Current group ticket kept
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-15 and `TG01` is dated 2026-10-15
- **THEN** `GROUPTICKET DELETE;TG01` is rejected and nothing is deleted

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Atomic deletions
A ticket or group ticket deletion that fails — because a rule rejects it or because the result cannot be stored — SHALL leave every ticket, internal ticket and pass exactly as before, both in the running application and in the stored data.

#### Scenario: Storage failure
- **WHEN** `GROUPTICKET DELETE;TG01` passes the rules but the data files cannot be written
- **THEN** an `ERROR` line is printed and `TG01`, its internal tickets and their passes all still exist
