# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Delete individual ticket
`TICKET DELETE;id` SHALL delete an individual ticket only when its date is later than the system date (the same day does not count), deleting all its SpeedPasses and its Special Pass as well. Tickets that are part of a group ticket SHALL NOT be deleted with this command. A rejected deletion SHALL leave the ticket and its passes unchanged.

#### Scenario: Future ticket deleted
- **WHEN** ticket T001 dated 2026-10-15 exists and the system date is 2026-10-14
- **THEN** `TICKET DELETE;T001` deletes it and prints `OK TICKET DELETED`

#### Scenario: Ticket for today or the past
- **WHEN** ticket T001 dated 2026-10-15 exists and the system date is 2026-10-15 or later
- **THEN** `TICKET DELETE;T001` prints an `ERROR` result and the ticket remains

#### Scenario: Internal ticket of a group ticket
- **WHEN** `TICKET DELETE` targets an individual ticket generated by a group ticket
- **THEN** an `ERROR` result is printed and the ticket remains

#### Scenario: Passes deleted in cascade
- **WHEN** future ticket T001 with SpeedPasses and a Special Pass is deleted
- **THEN** its passes no longer exist, also after a restart
