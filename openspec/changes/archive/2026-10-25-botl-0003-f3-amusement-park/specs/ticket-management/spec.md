# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Delete future ticket
`TICKET DELETE;id` SHALL delete an individual ticket only when its date is strictly later than the system date, deleting all its SpeedPasses and its Special Pass with it. Tickets that are part of a group ticket SHALL NOT be deleted individually.

#### Scenario: Future ticket deleted
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01 and `TICKET DELETE;T001` is processed for a ticket dated 2026-10-15
- **THEN** T001 is deleted

#### Scenario: Today's ticket kept
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-15 and `TICKET DELETE;T001` is processed for a ticket dated 2026-10-15
- **THEN** an `ERROR` is reported and T001 remains

#### Scenario: Internal ticket of a group ticket
- **WHEN** `TICKET DELETE` names an individual ticket generated by a group ticket
- **THEN** an `ERROR` is reported and the ticket remains

#### Scenario: Passes deleted with the ticket
- **WHEN** future ticket T001 has SpeedPasses and a Special Pass and `TICKET DELETE;T001` is processed
- **THEN** T001 and all its passes are deleted, also from storage
