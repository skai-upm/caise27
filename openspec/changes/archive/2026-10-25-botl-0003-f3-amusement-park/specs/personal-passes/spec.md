# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Sells personal passes attached to individual tickets: SpeedPasses for priority entry and a Special Pass for SPECIAL attractions, with refunds, consumption tracking and persistence.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Buy SpeedPasses
`SPEEDPASS CREATE;<ticketId>;<quantity>` SHALL accept quantity `1` or `10` for an existing individual ticket (bought directly or generated by a group ticket) dated on or after the system date, and SHALL create that many independent SpeedPasses, each with its own identifier (`<ticketId>-SP<n>`), price equal to 20% of the ticket's stored price, and an unused status. The reply SHALL show the amount charged. Other quantities, group ticket identifiers, unknown and past tickets SHALL be rejected.

#### Scenario: Pack of ten
- **WHEN** T001 costs 40.00 and `SPEEDPASS CREATE;T001;10` is executed
- **THEN** ten unused SpeedPasses of 8.00 each are attached to T001 and the reply shows 80.00

#### Scenario: Invalid quantity
- **WHEN** `SPEEDPASS CREATE;T001;5` is executed
- **THEN** an error is shown and no pass is created

### Requirement: Buy Special Pass
`SPECIALPASS CREATE;<ticketId>` SHALL create one Special Pass (`<ticketId>-SPECIAL`) priced at the ticket's stored price for an existing individual ticket dated on or after the system date, and SHALL show the amount charged. A second Special Pass for a ticket that already has one, consumed or not, SHALL be rejected.

#### Scenario: Only one Special Pass
- **WHEN** T001 already has a Special Pass and `SPECIALPASS CREATE;T001` is executed
- **THEN** an error is shown

### Requirement: Refund passes
`SPEEDPASS DELETE;<passId>` and `SPECIALPASS DELETE;<passId>` SHALL delete (refund) an unused pass of the corresponding kind when its ticket's date is strictly after the system date, showing the refunded amount. Consumed passes, passes of tickets dated on or before the system date and unknown identifiers SHALL be rejected.

#### Scenario: Refund unused
- **WHEN** the system date is 2026-10-01, T001 is dated 2026-10-15 and `SPEEDPASS DELETE;T001-SP1` is executed for an unused pass
- **THEN** the pass is removed and the refund amount is shown

#### Scenario: Consumed pass kept
- **WHEN** a pass has been consumed and its deletion is requested
- **THEN** an error is shown and the pass remains with consumed status

### Requirement: Pass persistence
Passes SHALL be stored in a text file next to the other data files with identifier, kind, ticket identifier, price and consumed status, and restored at startup attached to their tickets. Under `--fly` they SHALL be stored in the temporary directory.

#### Scenario: Passes restored
- **WHEN** T001 has one consumed SpeedPass and one unused Special Pass and the application restarts
- **THEN** both passes exist with the same prices and statuses
