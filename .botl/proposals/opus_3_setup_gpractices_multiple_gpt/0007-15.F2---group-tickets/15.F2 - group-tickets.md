## Authoritative milestone statement (verbatim)

### 17.4. F2-C04 — Group tickets

Nonempty groups may create tickets through GROUPTICKET CREATE, provided the specified date is not in the past. The group ticket must be composed of the individual tickets of the members included in the purchase. Their identifiers will be generated automatically using <groupTicketIdentifier>:<dni>. The group ticket must also maintain a reference to the purchasing group while it exists in the system and use the current system price as the basis for the applicable discount. This milestone must also add GROUPTICKET DELETE and GROUP DELETE. A group ticket may be deleted only when its date is strictly later than the current date. GROUP ADD, GROUP REMOVE, and GROUP DELETE must check whether associated active group tickets exist. The USER DELETE check must also cover individual tickets belonging to active group tickets and prevent deletion when it would modify a locked group.


## Authoritative detailed requirements (verbatim source excerpts)

## 5. User groups

Registered users may be organized into groups. Each group will have a unique identifier, a name, and will consist of users who already exist in the system. A group is created using:

GROUP CREATE;Identifier;Name

For example:

```text
GROUP CREATE;G01;Familia Garcia
```

To add an existing user to a group, use:

GROUP ADD;Identifier;Email

For example:

```text
GROUP ADD;G01;ana@correo.com
GROUP ADD;G01;pedro@correo.com
```

The same user may not appear more than once in the same group. GROUP ADD must reject the addition of a user who already belongs to that group. A group may be created empty and users added progressively. It must also be possible to remove a user from a group while the group is modifiable:

GROUP REMOVE;Identifier;Email

A group may also be deleted using:

GROUP DELETE;Identifier

Deletion is permitted only if there is no group ticket associated with that group whose date is equal to or later than the current system date. Group tickets for earlier dates alone will not prevent deletion of the group. If the group has a future ticket, it may first be deleted using GROUPTICKET DELETE. A ticket for the current day cannot be deleted and will continue to prevent deletion of the group. A group has an active group ticket when at least one associated group ticket has a date equal to or later than the current system date. GROUP ADD and GROUP REMOVE may be performed only when the group has no associated active group tickets. Group tickets for earlier dates are considered historical and will not prevent group modification. If a future ticket is deleted using GROUPTICKET DELETE, the application must check again whether other active group tickets remain associated with the group. If none remain, the group can be modified again. The group’s locked state must not be stored as an independent attribute; instead, it must be determined from the existing tickets and the current system date.

### 7.3. Group ticket

A group may purchase a joint ticket for all users who belong to it at that moment using:

GROUPTICKET CREATE;<identifier>;<group>;<date>

For example:

```text
GROUPTICKET CREATE;TG01;G01;2027-04-15
```

The identifier must be unique among all tickets in the system. The specified date must be equal to or later than the current system date; group tickets may not be created for past dates. A group must contain at least one user before a group ticket can be created. Empty groups may be created and populated progressively, but a group ticket may not be purchased while the group is empty. A group ticket must be composed of individual tickets, one for each user belonging to the group at purchase time. These individual tickets constitute the composition of the group ticket and must not be shared with other group tickets. The identifier of each individual ticket created as part of the composition will be generated automatically using:

<groupTicketIdentifier>:<dni>

For example, for a group ticket identified as TG01 and a user with DNI 12345678Z, the corresponding individual ticket will have the identifier:

```text
TG01:12345678Z
```

These derived identifiers belong to the same shared ticket identifier space and must be checked before group ticket creation is completed. Individual tickets belonging to a group ticket may not be deleted directly using TICKET DELETE. They may only be deleted as a consequence of valid deletion of the containing group ticket using GROUPTICKET DELETE. Each individual ticket comprising a group ticket will hold a reference to the corresponding user. The group ticket must not maintain a parallel collection of users duplicating this information. The base price used to create individual tickets will be the current price configured in the system. A discount based on the number of members included in the purchase will be applied to that price. Once the group ticket is successfully created, the application must display the total purchase price after applying the corresponding discount. The composition created at purchase time must not subsequently change, even if the group’s state changes. A group ticket must hold a reference to the group that made the purchase while that group exists in the system. This relationship is unidirectional: the group ticket knows its group, but the group must not hold references to purchased tickets.

If the group is subsequently deleted under the established rules, the reference held by historical group tickets may become null. Similarly, users must not hold references to their tickets. Whenever it is necessary to determine the tickets associated with a user or group, this information must be obtained by traversing the tickets managed by the system. A group ticket may be deleted using:

GROUPTICKET DELETE;<identifier>

Deletion is permitted only when the group ticket date is strictly later than the current system date. Group tickets for the current day or earlier dates may not be deleted and must be retained as historical records. When a future group ticket is deleted, the individual tickets in its composition must also be deleted.

## 8. Group discounts

The applicable discount depends on the number of users included in the group ticket when it is created. The following brackets apply:

| Number of members | Discount |
| --- | --- |
| Fewer than 3 | 0% |
| 3 to 5 | 10% |
| 6 to 10 | 20% |
| More than 10 | 50% |

The final price must be calculated by reusing the common behavior defined for tickets and applying the rules specific to group tickets. The discount will be applied to the base price of each individual ticket in the group ticket. The total group ticket price may be obtained from the prices of its constituent individual tickets. For example, if the current system price is 40 euros and a group of 6 users creates a group ticket:

```text
GROUPTICKET CREATE;TG02;G02;2027-04-15
```

a discount of 20% will be applied. Each of the six individual tickets in the group ticket will cost 32 euros, for a total of 192 euros. In this case, the application must display a total charge of 192 euros. As long as any associated active group ticket exists, the group composition will remain locked against modification. Once all associated tickets are historical or have been validly deleted, the group may be modified again.

## 9. Relationship between users and tickets

A ticket is considered active when its date is equal to or later than the current system date. Tickets with earlier dates are considered historical. From this delivery onward, USER DELETE, defined in Phase 1, must take account of existing tickets. A user may not be deleted while any active ticket is associated with them. This restriction applies both to individual tickets created using TICKET CREATE and to individual tickets comprising a group ticket. Historical tickets alone will not prevent deletion of the user. A user’s locked state must not be stored as an independent attribute, but derived from existing tickets and the current system date. If the user only has future tickets, those tickets may first be deleted through the corresponding deletion operations. Tickets for the current day may not be deleted and will continue to block USER DELETE. Users must not store their own tickets. To determine a user’s tickets, the information must be obtained from the tickets managed by the system. When deleting a user is permitted, the system must remove that user from the groups they belong to in order to maintain model consistency. However, USER DELETE may not be performed if the user belongs to a group locked by an associated active group ticket. Deleting the user would indirectly modify the composition of that group and must therefore respect the same restrictions that prevent GROUP ADD or GROUP REMOVE when an active group ticket exists. If all groups to which the user belongs can be modified, their removal from those groups will occur as an internal consequence of USER DELETE. Historical group tickets will preserve the composition they had at purchase time. Deleting a user must not invalidate the historical information of tickets with dates earlier than the current date. Deletion changes the user’s status as an active system user, but historical tickets must be retained as fiscal records. Persistence must preserve the minimum information needed to correctly reconstruct those tickets even if the user no longer exists as an active user. Likewise, deleting a group must not invalidate historical group tickets with earlier dates. When a group is deleted, its associated historical group tickets must be retained as fiscal records. The group reference may become null, while the group ticket retains the information needed to preserve that historical record, including its identifier, date, price, and composition.
