## Why

Users and groups need on-demand accounting views assembled without duplicating purchases or forcing irrelevant invoice sections.

## What changes

- Implement `INVOICE CREATE;USER;<correo>` and `INVOICE CREATE;GROUP;<identificadorGrupo>`.
- Construct invoice objects using a common Builder abstraction and differentiated construction steps.
- Show applicable charges and totals; exclude duplicated component-ticket charges and omit empty sections.

## Scope boundaries

- No requirement to persist generated invoice objects.
- Do not charge constituent tickets twice or include members’ personal passes in group totals.

## Proposed requirements for delta specs

- **R1:** The system SHALL generate a user invoice only for an existing active user and a group invoice only for an existing group.
- **R2:** Invoice construction SHALL explicitly use the Builder pattern with a common abstraction and distinct steps.
- **R3:** A user invoice SHALL include relevant directly purchased individual tickets and applicable personally purchased passes, with item prices and the total.
- **R4:** A user invoice SHALL NOT count constituent individual tickets of group purchases as independent ticket purchases.
- **R5:** A group invoice SHALL include applicable group-ticket purchases and their totals without separately counting constituent individual tickets or personal passes.
- **R6:** Invoices SHALL omit sections for which no applicable information exists and SHALL be generated on demand.

## Key acceptance scenarios

### S1 — User invoice without double counting
- **Given** a user owns a directly purchased ticket and is also included in a group-ticket composition
- **When** `INVOICE CREATE;USER;<correo>` executes
- **Then** the direct ticket is charged once and the constituent ticket is not charged as another ticket purchase

### S2 — Personal passes
- **Given** a user has a personal pass attached to a constituent group ticket
- **When** a user invoice is generated
- **Then** the personally purchased pass can appear without counting its group ticket twice

### S3 — Group invoice
- **Given** a group has purchased a group ticket and members have personal passes
- **When** `INVOICE CREATE;GROUP;<identificadorGrupo>` executes
- **Then** the group-ticket amount appears and members’ personal passes are not added to the group total

### S4 — Empty sections
- **Given** a recipient has no purchases in a given category
- **When** the invoice is constructed
- **Then** the irrelevant section is omitted

### S5 — Unknown recipient
- **Given** the requested user or group does not exist
- **When** invoice creation is requested
- **Then** the operation fails in a controlled manner without generating a misleading invoice

````text
# F3-C02 — Invoice generation using Builder

"Objective
Introduce invoice generation for users and groups by formally applying the Builder pattern.
Description
The application must generate invoices on demand from the financial information available in the system.
The following commands will be added:
INVOICE CREATE;USER;<correo>
INVOICE CREATE;GROUP;<identificadorGrupo>
An invoice must be built progressively using Builder, including only sections applicable to the recipient type and available information.
A user invoice may include individual tickets purchased directly for that user and personal passes associated with their tickets. Individual tickets belonging to a GROUPTICKET must not be counted again as individual purchases, although personal passes attached to them may appear.
A group invoice will include its GROUPTICKET purchases and their total amount, without adding the individual tickets composing them or the members' personal passes again.
Invoices are generated on demand and need not be persisted.
Acceptance criteria
• 
A Builder abstraction exists for constructing invoices.
• 
An invoice can be generated for an existing user.
• 
An invoice can be generated for an existing group.
• 
The invoice is assembled in distinct steps.
• 
Only sections applicable to the recipient and available data are included.
• 
Constructors with numerous optional parameters are not used to handle invoice variants.
• 
Internal tickets of a GROUPTICKET are not counted twice.
• 
Personal passes associated with internal tickets may appear on the user's invoice.
• 
Invoices display the prices of included items and a total.
• 
Invoices do not need to be persisted.
Mandatory commit
[F3-C02] Add invoice generation with builder"
````
