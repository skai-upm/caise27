## Why

Attraction entry now needs pass-aware authorization while ensuring failed operations never consume purchases or partially admit groups.

## What changes

- Extend `ATTRACTION ENTER` with optional `;SPEED`.
- Consume one available SpeedPass for valid individual entry; for valid group entry consume one from each constituent ticket atomically.
- Require and automatically consume an available Special Pass for SPECIAL attractions; reject SPEED there.

## Scope boundaries

- Do not weaken existing date, capacity, or ticket-type checks.
- Do not consume passes if any access precondition fails.

## Proposed requirements for delta specs

- **R1:** The normal two-parameter `ATTRACTION ENTER` SHALL remain available.
- **R2:** For individual entry with `SPEED`, the system SHALL require an unconsumed SpeedPass and SHALL consume exactly one only after successful access.
- **R3:** For group entry with `SPEED`, each constituent ticket SHALL have an unconsumed SpeedPass and admission/consumption SHALL be all-or-nothing.
- **R4:** SPECIAL entry SHALL accept an individual ticket only, require an unconsumed Special Pass, and consume it automatically on successful access.
- **R5:** SPECIAL attractions SHALL reject the `SPEED` option.
- **R6:** Successful consumption SHALL identify consumed passes in the command output.

## Key acceptance scenarios

### S1 — Single SpeedPass
- **Given** an eligible ticket has multiple unused SpeedPasses
- **When** it successfully enters with SPEED
- **Then** exactly one is consumed and identified

### S2 — Group atomicity
- **Given** a group has at least one member without a SpeedPass
- **When** it requests SPEED entry
- **Then** no group member enters and no passes are consumed

### S3 — Capacity failure
- **Given** a group has passes but lacks attraction capacity
- **When** it requests SPEED entry
- **Then** no entry or consumption occurs

### S4 — Special entry
- **Given** a valid individual ticket has an unused Special Pass
- **When** it enters SPECIAL without SPEED
- **Then** the Special Pass is consumed automatically and reported

### S5 — Special SPEED rejection
- **Given** entry to SPECIAL specifies SPEED
- **When** the command executes
- **Then** the request is rejected and pass states remain unchanged

# F3-C08 — Attraction access using passes

"Objective
Extend ATTRACTION ENTER to use SpeedPasses and Special Passes while respecting atomicity, attraction type, and consumption rules.
Description
At GENERAL and INDIVIDUAL attractions, an individual ticket may enter normally or request use of a SpeedPass with the SPEED option.
GENERAL and GROUP attractions also permit group ticket entry with SPEED. In that case, every individual ticket in the composition must have at least one unused SpeedPass. If any member lacks one or any other access condition fails, no user enters and no SpeedPass is consumed.
SPECIAL attractions permit individual entries only. The system automatically locates the Special Pass associated with the ticket, checks that it has not been consumed, and marks it as consumed only after successful entry. The SPEED option is not accepted at these attractions.
Acceptance criteria
• 
ATTRACTION ENTER;<atraccion>;<entrada> preserves normal entry.
• 
ATTRACTION ENTER;<atraccion>;<entrada>;SPEED requests SpeedPass use.
• 
Individual entry with SPEED consumes exactly one unused SpeedPass.
• 
Group entry with SPEED consumes one for each member.
• 
Group entry with SPEED is atomic.
• 
A failed entry does not consume passes.
• 
A SPECIAL attraction automatically consumes its Special Pass.
• 
The output reports when a Special Pass or SpeedPass is consumed.
• 
SPEED is rejected at SPECIAL attractions.
Mandatory commit
[F3-C08] Extend attraction access and pass consumption"
