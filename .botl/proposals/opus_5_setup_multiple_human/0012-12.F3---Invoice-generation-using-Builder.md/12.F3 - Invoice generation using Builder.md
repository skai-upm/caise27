# F3-C02 — Invoice generation using Builder

"Objective
Introduce invoice generation for users and groups by formally applying the Builder pattern.
Description
The application must generate invoices on demand from the financial information available in the system.
The following commands will be added:
INVOICE CREATE;USER;<correo>
INVOICE CREATE;GROUP;<identificadorGrupo>
An invoice must be built progressively using Builder, including only sections applicable to the recipient type and available information.
A user invoice may include individual tickets purchased directly for that user and personal passes associated with their tickets. Individual tickets belonging to a GROUPTICKET must not be counted again as individual purchases, although personal passes attached to them may appear.
A group invoice will include its GROUPTICKET purchases and their total amount, without adding the individual tickets composing them or the members' personal passes again.
Invoices are generated on demand and need not be persisted.
Acceptance criteria
• 
A Builder abstraction exists for constructing invoices.
• 
An invoice can be generated for an existing user.
• 
An invoice can be generated for an existing group.
• 
The invoice is assembled in distinct steps.
• 
Only sections applicable to the recipient and available data are included.
• 
Constructors with numerous optional parameters are not used to handle invoice variants.
• 
Internal tickets of a GROUPTICKET are not counted twice.
• 
Personal passes associated with internal tickets may appear on the user's invoice.
• 
Invoices display the prices of included items and a total.
• 
Invoices do not need to be persisted.
Mandatory commit
[F3-C02] Add invoice generation with builder"
