# F3-C06 — Introduce SpeedPasses and Special Passes

"Objective
Add personal passes associated with individual tickets, including prices, persistence, consumption, and refunds.
Description
An individual ticket may have multiple SpeedPasses and at most one Special Pass throughout its lifetime.
SpeedPasses may be purchased individually or in packs of ten. Each SpeedPass is an independent object with an identifier, price, and consumption status. Its price is 20% of the price stored in its associated individual ticket.
The Special Pass costs the same as the price stored in its associated individual ticket. Only one may be purchased for a given individual ticket, even if it has subsequently been used.
Consumed passes remain in the system with their corresponding status. Unused passes may be refunded when the date of the associated ticket permits a refund under E2's time-related rules.
Acceptance criteria
• 
SPEEDPASS CREATE accepts quantities of 1 and 10.
• 
A purchase of ten generates ten independent SpeedPasses.
• 
Each SpeedPass stores its own price and consumption status.
• 
SPECIALPASS CREATE permits at most one Special Pass per individual ticket.
• 
A SpeedPass costs 20% of the associated ticket price.
• 
The Special Pass price equals the associated ticket price.
• 
Purchases display the amount charged.
• 
SPEEDPASS DELETE and SPECIALPASS DELETE allow unused passes to be refunded when permitted.
• 
Consumed passes are not removed upon use.
Mandatory commit
[F3-C06] Add speed and special passes"
