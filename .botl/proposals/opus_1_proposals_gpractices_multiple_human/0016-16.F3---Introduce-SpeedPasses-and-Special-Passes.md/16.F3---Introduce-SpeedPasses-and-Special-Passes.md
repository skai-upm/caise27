## Why

Individual tickets need optional personal add-ons with their own purchase, refund, pricing, and consumption state.

## What changes

- Introduce SpeedPass and Special Pass entities associated with individual tickets, including constituent tickets of group purchases.
- Support purchase and refund commands, quantities 1 or 10 for SpeedPasses, and exactly one lifetime Special Pass per individual ticket.
- Calculate prices from the associated ticket’s stored price and preserve consumption state.

## Scope boundaries

- Passes are tied to individual tickets, not directly to users.
- Do not implement attraction-specific consumption/entry rules before the dedicated access change.
- Persistence and cascade deletion are expanded in proposal 9; define the relevant data contracts now.

## Proposed requirements for delta specs

- **R1:** `SPEEDPASS CREATE;<purchaseIdentifier>;<individualTicket>;<quantity>` SHALL accept only quantity 1 or 10 and create separate pass objects with derived identifiers.
- **R2:** `SPECIALPASS CREATE;<identifier>;<individualTicket>` SHALL allow no more than one Special Pass for a ticket over its lifetime, including when consumed.
- **R3:** A SpeedPass SHALL cost 20% of its associated individual ticket’s stored price; a Special Pass SHALL cost 100% of that price.
- **R4:** Each pass SHALL record identifier, price, associated ticket, and consumed status.
- **R5:** A successful pass purchase SHALL display its total price and SHALL create no partial purchase if an identifier conflicts.
- **R6:** `SPEEDPASS DELETE` and `SPECIALPASS DELETE` SHALL allow a refund only for an unconsumed pass whose associated ticket is strictly in the future.
- **R7:** Consumed passes SHALL remain recorded and SHALL NOT be refundable.

## Key acceptance scenarios

### S1 — SpeedPass pack
- **Given** a valid current or future individual ticket exists
- **When** a SpeedPass purchase of quantity 10 is made
- **Then** ten distinct passes are created and the total is ten times the single-pass price without an extra discount

### S2 — Disallowed quantity
- **Given** a purchase requests another quantity
- **When** `SPEEDPASS CREATE` executes
- **Then** nothing is purchased

### S3 — Identifier conflict
- **Given** a proposed pack would generate an existing pass identifier
- **When** the pack is requested
- **Then** no partial passes are created

### S4 — Special Pass uniqueness
- **Given** a ticket already had a Special Pass, including a consumed one
- **When** another purchase is requested
- **Then** the purchase is rejected

### S5 — Pass refund
- **Given** a pass is unconsumed and its ticket date is after the system date
- **When** the pass is deleted
- **Then** the refund succeeds; but consumed, current-day and historical passes are rejected

### S6 — Discounted ticket
- **Given** an individual ticket belongs to a discounted group ticket
- **When** a pass is purchased
- **Then** the pass price is based on that ticket’s stored discounted price

# F3-C06 — Introduce SpeedPasses and Special Passes

"Objective
Add personal passes associated with individual tickets, including prices, persistence, consumption, and refunds.
Description
An individual ticket may have multiple SpeedPasses and at most one Special Pass throughout its lifetime.
SpeedPasses may be purchased individually or in packs of ten. Each SpeedPass is an independent object with an identifier, price, and consumption status. Its price is 20% of the price stored in its associated individual ticket.
The Special Pass costs the same as the price stored in its associated individual ticket. Only one may be purchased for a given individual ticket, even if it has subsequently been used.
Consumed passes remain in the system with their corresponding status. Unused passes may be refunded when the date of the associated ticket permits a refund under E2's time-related rules.
Acceptance criteria
• 
SPEEDPASS CREATE accepts quantities of 1 and 10.
• 
A purchase of ten generates ten independent SpeedPasses.
• 
Each SpeedPass stores its own price and consumption status.
• 
SPECIALPASS CREATE permits at most one Special Pass per individual ticket.
• 
A SpeedPass costs 20% of the associated ticket price.
• 
The Special Pass price equals the associated ticket price.
• 
Purchases display the amount charged.
• 
SPEEDPASS DELETE and SPECIALPASS DELETE allow unused passes to be refunded when permitted.
• 
Consumed passes are not removed upon use.
Mandatory commit
[F3-C06] Add speed and special passes"
