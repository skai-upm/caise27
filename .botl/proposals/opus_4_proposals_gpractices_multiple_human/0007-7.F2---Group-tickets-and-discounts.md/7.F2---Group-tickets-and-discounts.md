# F2 - Group tickets and discounts

Objective: Allow groups to purchase tickets jointly, introducing composition, ticket specialization, and more complex business rules.
Description:
A group may purchase a group ticket for a specified date.
Buying a group ticket must internally generate an individual ticket for each user who belongs to the group at the time of purchase.
A group ticket is therefore composed of the individual tickets corresponding to its members.
The individual price set for the purchase will be used to calculate the total price. A discount based on the number of members will be applied to the sum of individual tickets:

Members
Discount

Fewer than 3
0%

3–5
10%

6–10
20%

More than 10
50%
For example, for an individual ticket costing €40:2 people -> €80 -> 0% -> €80
3 people -> €120 -> 10% -> €108
6 people -> €240 -> 20% -> €192
11 people -> €440 -> 50% -> €220
A possible command is:GROUPTICKET CREATE;TG01;G01;2026-10-15
The group composition used is the one existing at the time of purchase.
As long as a group has any group ticket dated on or after SYSTEM DATE, the group is locked. Members cannot be added or removed, and the group itself cannot be deleted.
Likewise, the generated individual tickets prevent their associated users from being deleted while those tickets are current or future.
When all associated tickets are in the past, the group can be modified again.
The base price will be that of an individual ticket.
On success, the command will return:OK GROUP TICKET CREATED -> <Precio>
We also add:GROUPTICKET DELETE;TG001
Deletion is permitted only when the ticket date is in the future (later than the system date), and all internal individual tickets of the group's members associated with that group ticket will be deleted.
